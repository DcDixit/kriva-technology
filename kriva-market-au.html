Operating conditions

What makes Australian builds different.

Australian transport operators carry legal obligations through the whole supply chain, and finance teams overwhelmingly run on Xero. Software that ignores either one becomes a second job for the people using it. We build the screens and data model that record these obligations; we do not give legal or tax advice, and your compliance owner signs off the rules.

  • Heavy Vehicle National Law and Chain of Responsibility records
  • Fatigue management and work diary data beside dispatch
  • Xero-first invoicing, with GST coded for BAS reporting
  • Separate rules where WA and NT run their own heavy vehicle regimes
  • Privacy Act-conscious access control and audit trails

National operators, state differences

A rule change is a setting, not a rebuild.

The national law applies in most states and territories, but Western Australia and the Northern Territory run their own heavy vehicle regimes. Operators running interstate need rules configurable by jurisdiction, not hard-coded once. We model them as data, so a rule change is a setting, not a rebuild.

Chain of Responsibility

Chain of Responsibility evidence

Under the Heavy Vehicle National Law, schedulers, loaders, and consignors can share responsibility for a breach, not only the driver. The useful software response is a record: who planned the job, which hours and mass limits were checked, and when. We build that trail into the dispatch workflow instead of a separate form nobody completes.

Fatigue

Fatigue and work diary data

Fatigue rules decide whether a driver can take the next job. Where your electronic work diary or telematics provider offers an API, we show remaining work and rest time on the assignment view, so the desk respects the limit at planning time instead of discovering it at a roadside check.

Finance

Xero and BAS-ready records

Xero is widely used by Australian small and mid-size businesses. We sync invoices, bills, and settlements through Xero’s official API with GST coded correctly at the source, so BAS preparation starts from clean records, and every synced or failed item is logged with a retry path.
