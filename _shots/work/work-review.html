Full-page captures of the current page (git HEAD) and the redesign, at 1440px and 390px. Use the toolbar to switch width and to view one version at full width. Captures are taken after scrolling the whole page once, so every section has revealed.

Changed in this review pass

  • Calmer motion: card tilt cut roughly in half, the hover lift is smaller, and there's no scale-up on hover.
  • Headings: "Product case studies" and "Websites we designed" now use the same heading scale as the CRM page.
  • Filter bar sits flush under the header at every width. It used to slide 7–15px under it, a carry-over from the old page.
  • Mobile filters: a tapped chip slides to the middle of the row, so the next options stay in reach.
  • Mobile cards: number badges no longer overlap the mock-ups, and the CRMPulse stage labels no longer collide.
  • Spacing: tighter gaps between the groups.
