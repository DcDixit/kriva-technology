Operating conditions

What makes Canadian builds different.

Canadian operators deal with provincial variation and cross-border US traffic that a US-built product rarely handles well. The friction shows up in tax lines, language, and border paperwork. We build the screens and data model that record these obligations; we do not give legal or tax advice, and your compliance owner signs off the rules.

  • GST, HST, PST, and QST handled per province on invoices
  • Cross-border US loads with eManifest status on the record
  • Federal ELD data alongside dispatch for federally regulated carriers
  • French and English interfaces where Quebec users need them
  • PIPEDA-conscious access control and audit trails

Cross-border dispatch

Toronto to Chicago, every status explicit.

A Toronto–Chicago load needs an eManifest filed before the truck reaches the border, and a customs hold is not the same problem as a late driver. We track border and paperwork status as explicit states on the load, so the desk can tell what is actually blocking a delivery and chase the right party.

In depth

Built for more than one province and one language.

Sales tax

Provincial sales tax on every invoice

An Ontario invoice carries HST, a Quebec invoice carries GST and QST, and other provinces apply GST with or without a provincial tax depending on what is sold. When this is a manual line edit, invoices go out wrong. We model the tax rule by province and sync the result to QuickBooks or Xero, so finance reviews exceptions instead of every invoice.

Accounting integrations

Hours

ELD data where dispatch can use it

Federally regulated Canadian carriers must use certified ELDs. That data is far more useful on the dispatch screen than in a separate portal. Where your ELD provider offers an API, we show remaining hours on the assignment row, so planning respects the limit rather than discovering it mid-trip.

Dispatch CRM

Language

Bilingual interfaces for Quebec

Supporting French is a layout and content-structure problem before it is a translation problem: French strings run longer, and labels, tables, and buttons need room for that. We build components and content keys for both languages from the first wireframe, and your team or a translation partner supplies the copy.
