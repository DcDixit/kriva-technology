Best for

Ops and support drowning in the same steps

Teams who already know the repeatable work, and need a person on anything that reaches a customer or the ledger.

What we own

Audit, build, gates, fallback

Workflow map, Make or Zapier (or custom), human approval on send, and monitoring so failed runs return to a queue.

Not this service

Connectors, close UX, or “AI to ship code”

Idempotent APIs live under Integrations. Month-end reconciliation lives on QuickBooks & Xero. Using AI to design and build product lives under AI-Assisted Development.

The loop

When the same steps burn every shift.

  1. 01 · Map

    Trigger, system, exception

    We write the loop as it actually runs, including the spreadsheet and the Slack ping, before we automate it.

  2. 02 · Gate

    A human on send

    Customer-facing or ledger-facing steps get a review gate. Internal triage can run darker.

  3. 03 · Own

    Your team after launch

    Docs, rollback, and who changes the scenario. The workflow does not remain a KRIVA black box.

FAQ

About Automation Workflows

Asked most often before a first call when the brief mixes Make, Zapier, and a person on send.

Ask us directly→

Make and Zapier are the default when they fit. We write custom workers when the platform is the bottleneck, the volume is too high, or the gate needs a console the iPaaS cannot host. The engagement starts with the loop, not the logo.

Automation owns the queue: triggers, human gates, and fallback. Integrations owns connector architecture, idempotency, webhooks, and error surfaces. Use this page when a person is still in the loop on send. Use Integrations when two systems must stay in agreement without a nightly spreadsheet.

AI-Assisted Development is how KRIVA ships design and code faster. This service is the customer’s ops queue: ticket triage, drafts, invoice triggers, with a human on send. We may use an LLM inside a Make scenario. That is not the same as using AI to build your product.

Yes. We chain LLM steps into Make or custom workers for summarization, routing, and draft generation, with approval gates throughout. Low-confidence output falls back to a person. Nothing customer-facing sends on its own.

Automation owns the trigger and the gate. The write into QuickBooks Online or Xero uses the original APIs under QuickBooks & Xero integrations, not a CSV export after the run. We pair the two services when the queue and the ledger both matter.

Your team. We leave a map, named scenarios, monitoring, and a fallback queue so the pipeline survives the original builder. Maintenance can stay in-house, or we can retain a light ops retainer if you ask for it.
