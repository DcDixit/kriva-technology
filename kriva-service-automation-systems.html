Make, Zapier & custom automation

Automation with a person on send.

Human gates on send · You own the scenarios

Automation workflows with human approval gates for ops and support teams

Who it's for

Ops and support teams drowning in the same steps every shift. Teams who already know the repeatable work, and need a person on anything that reaches a customer or the ledger.

What we deliver

Workflow map, Make or Zapier (or custom), human approval on send, and monitoring so failed runs return to a queue. Audit, build, gates, fallback — owned end to end.

Not this service

Idempotent APIs live under Integrations. Month-end reconciliation lives on QuickBooks & Xero. Using AI to design and build product lives under AI-Assisted Development.

Kriva maps the loop first, then automates with a human on send. You own the scenarios, docs, and monitoring after launch.

Silent failures

Failed runs disappear into logs. Operators find out when a customer complains or an invoice never fires.COST: missed SLAs, manual catch-up, brittle one-person Zaps

The loop

When the same steps burn every shift.

We write the loop as it actually runs — including the spreadsheet and the Slack ping — before we automate it.

  1. 01 · Map

    Trigger, system, exception

    Document the real handoffs: what fires the run, which system owns the record, and where operators already intervene.

  2. 02 · Gate

    A human on send

    Customer-facing or ledger-facing steps get a review gate. Internal triage can run darker, with confidence thresholds and fallback.

  3. 03 · Own

    Your team after launch

    Docs, rollback, and who changes the scenario. The workflow does not remain a KRIVA black box.

Platforms

Make, Zapier, or custom — chosen for the job.

The engagement starts with the loop, not the logo. We pick the platform that fits volume, gates, and who owns it after launch.

Make & Zapier

Default when volume, connectors, and review gates fit inside the iPaaS. Fast to iterate, clear scenario ownership for your team.

Make Zapier
  • Ticket triage, routing, and draft generation
  • Webhook triggers with visible error states

Custom workers

When the platform is the bottleneck, volume is too high, or the gate needs a console the iPaaS cannot host.

  • Queue-backed workers with idempotent retries
  • LLM steps with confidence scoring and fallback
  • Operator consoles for approve, hold, and re-run

Human gates & monitoring

Approve and Hold are first-class. Failed runs return to a queue — not a log file nobody checks.

  • Review queues for send, approve, and ledger steps
  • Low-confidence output falls back to a person
  • Monitoring, rollback paths, and named scenario owners
