When opinions replace evidence

The loudest stakeholder is not a sample.

We shadow operators, test the paths people actually take, and bring a readout the team can act on, not a 60-page appendix.

The sprint

Most focused studies run 2–4 weeks.

  1. 01 · Shadow

    Ops and first-run

    Sit with the desks that live in the product. Activation research for SaaS; floor shadowing for ops tools.

  2. 02 · Test

    Usability on the real path

    Tasks against the current product or a prototype. We record where people stall, not whether they “like” the UI.

  3. 03 · Readout

    Decisions, not theatre

    Journey maps and a ranked list of what to change before UI is locked. Recruitment and scope set the calendar.
