Custom dashboard design and development for operational and SaaS teams

Who it's for

Ops, management, finance, and service teams drowning in data. Carriers, SaaS products, and service orgs where reporting exists but the morning scan still happens in a spreadsheet — or every role sees the same cluttered screen.

What we deliver

Custom dashboard design and development. Operational dashboards, admin panels, and reporting views with clear hierarchy, role-based filters, data visualization, and integration with CRMs, ERPs, APIs, and databases you already run.

Problems we solve

Scattered data, weak signals, spreadsheet workarounds. When charts compete for attention, filters don't reach the right records, or exports replace action — we map the decision first, then design the view that moves users from information to action.

Kriva is a product design and engineering studio — not a dashboard product vendor. You own the designs, code, and documentation after launch.

Dashboard problems

The data exists. The decision is still difficult.

Users see charts but cannot prioritize. Filters do not reach the right records. Actions require context switching or exporting.

Decision flow

Question → Signal → Relevant Records → Action.

Every dashboard answers one primary question. The signal shows what requires attention. Records provide evidence. Actions let users complete work without leaving the view.

  1. 01 · Question

    What must you decide now?

    Which routes are at SLA risk today?

  2. 02 · Signal

    What needs attention first?

    3 loads unowned and at risk appear above the fold.

  3. 03 · Records

    What evidence explains it?

    Each load shows lane, owner status, and time remaining.

  4. 04 · Action

    What can you do here?

    Assign owner or resolve — inline, without context switching.

Role-based views

The same data should not create the same screen for every role.

Operations, management, finance, and sales/service teams answer different questions. Hierarchy, KPIs, filters, and actions are shaped to fit — not duplicated across roles.

Operations: Exceptions and SLA risk first

  • Exceptions and SLA risk above the fold—not buried in a report
  • Owners and priorities visible on every row
  • Filters lead directly to actionable records
  • Inline assignment, reassignment, and resolution

Management: One primary signal, drill-down to evidence

  • One primary KPI or risk count at the top
  • Short trend or forecast below—not twelve competing charts
  • Exception count as the actionable element
  • Drill-down to operational records without leaving context

Finance: Reconciliation status and exception queues

  • Sync status and posting exceptions surfaced first
  • Filters by entity, period, and reconciliation state
  • Row-level detail for audit and investigation
  • Links to source records in accounting systems

Sales / Service: Pipeline health and next actions

  • Pipeline stage density and stalled deals above the fold
  • Rep-level filters and ownership on every row
  • Next-action prompts—not passive record lists
  • Activity and forecast trends as supporting context
