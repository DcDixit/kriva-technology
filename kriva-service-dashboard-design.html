Information architecture

Hierarchy first. Charts second.

Name the question. Rank the layout. Show the signal, then the evidence, then let the action finish in the same view.

  1. 01

    Questions

    What must someone know or decide in the first 60–90 seconds? Stakeholder chart requests wait until that answer sits above the fold.

Dashboard UX

Ops, admin, and exec are not the same view

The desk needs exceptions. Admin needs a task they can finish. Leadership needs one answer and a way to drill down.

What requires attention right now?

Exception queue with owners, SLA remaining, and an action on the row. Historical volume does not belong here.

  • Unowned and at-risk rows first
  • Filters that resolve to a record
  • Assign without leaving the queue

What needs to be configured or resolved?

Users, roles, and pending invites in a table an operator can finish — search, status, and the next action on the row.

  • Search and inline actions, not nested menus
  • Errors that say what to do next
  • Overrides visible, not buried in settings

What changed, and where should I drill down?

One primary metric, a short trend, and the exception count as the call to action. Not the operator’s density.

  • One answer, not twelve tiles
  • Exception count as the next step
  • Drill into ops evidence, not a new export
