Overview#

We collect only the information needed to respond to inquiries, deliver services, and improve our website. We do not sell personal data.

Information we collect#

When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, company name, project details, budget range, timeline, and any message you provide. We use Google Analytics 4 to collect page URLs, referrers, device and browser type, and interaction events such as button clicks and form submissions. Analytics cookies may include _ga and _ga_*. Microsoft Clarity may record how a session moves through a page. We do not collect advertising identifiers or use ads personalization.

How we use information#

We use contact submissions to evaluate fit, prepare proposals, schedule calls, and communicate about active engagements. We use analytics to understand which pages, sources, and calls-to-action help people find the right service, and to measure inquiries. We do not sell this data.

Third-party services#

Our site uses hosting (Vercel) and email delivery (Google Workspace and Gmail) to process contact form submissions. If that route is unavailable, your browser may send your submission through a backup email service (Web3Forms). Our team is based in India, and these providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. We use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to measure site usage and Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft) for session recordings. Links to third-party sites are governed by those sites' policies. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we disable analytics storage and do not load session recording.

Data retention#

We retain inquiry records for as long as needed to respond, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of marketing-related data where applicable law allows.

Your rights#

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to certain uses. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Security#

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Contact#

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form or email hello@krivatechnologies.com.

Data controller#

The data controller for this website is KRIVA Technologies, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. Email hello@krivatechnologies.com. Website https://krivatechnologies.com.

Your rights (UK GDPR)#

If UK data protection law applies to you, you can ask KRIVA Technologies for access to the personal data we hold, and for correction, deletion, or restriction. You can also object to certain processing, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Email hello@krivatechnologies.com to make a request.

California residents (CCPA)#

If you live in California, you can email hello@krivatechnologies.com to ask what personal information KRIVA Technologies holds about you, or to ask us to delete it. We do not sell personal information.
