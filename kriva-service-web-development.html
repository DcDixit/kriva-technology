Web development services: marketing sites, CMS, and performance baselines

Who it's for

Marketing and growth teams whose site feels dated, slow, or impossible to update. Founders who need a public surface that converts and ranks — not a brochure that waits on engineering for every copy change.

What we deliver

Marketing websites and public web surfaces in Next.js, React, and TypeScript. Reusable components, CMS when marketing must publish independently, and a performance and SEO baseline you can measure after launch.

Problems we solve

Dated presence next to the category, landing pages blocked on engineering tickets, and mobile paths that leak leads. We ship the site marketing can run — authenticated product UX lives on a different service.

Kriva is a product design and engineering studio — not a theme shop. You own the code, components, and CMS configuration after launch.
