Custom trucking and TMS software development for carriers and brokers

Who it's for

Carriers, brokers, and ops teams outgrowing their TMS. Dispatch desks where loads actually move differently than the vendor imagined, and the software needs to catch up.

What we deliver

Custom TMS development and modernization. Dispatcher consoles, driver and carrier interfaces, management portals, and integrations, built beside your existing stack or as a full rebuild.

Problems we solve

Spreadsheet dispatch, late exceptions, brittle integrations. When a workaround runs the shift, we map the real workflow first, then build the surface or connector that closes the gap.

Kriva is a product design and engineering studio, not a boxed TMS vendor. You own the code, designs, and documentation after launch.

Operational problems

The workaround runs the operation.

What we build

One load record. Three surfaces.

Every handoff, from tender to settlement, lives on a single load record, served to each team through the interface built for their job.

  1. 01

    Load created

    Customer order, lane, and SLA captured once, not re-keyed into a side sheet.

  2. 02

    Dispatch

    Assignment, bulk actions, and exceptions on one board with audit history.

  3. 03

    Driver / carrier

    Acceptance, in-transit updates, and documents, even on weak yard signal.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    POD, detention, and delivery confirmation tied back to the load row.

  5. 05

    Billing / settlement

    Invoice-ready data synced to finance with retry states finance can trust.

Dispatcher workspace

Bulk assignment, row-level SLA, and audited overrides for high-volume dispatch. Multi-select board with one assign action. Exception queue sorted by SLA risk.

Driver & carrier interface

Acceptance, stop progression, and POD on weak signal, one-handed in the yard. One-tap load acceptance. Queued updates and POD sync offline.

Management portal

Morning scan, lane performance, and exception oversight before the customer calls. Exception queue above the fold. Seven-day lane performance.

How we work

Configure, extend, or rebuild
without stopping the dispatch desk.

We assess the workflow before recommending a build approach. Custom is a conclusion, not a default.

  1. Configure

    Improve what already works

    Your TMS handles core workflows and integrations are stable. Tune fields, permissions, reports, or minor workflow gaps without a platform project.

  2. Extend

    Add layers beside the stack

    Keep the TMS as source of record. Add a dispatch console, driver app, customer portal, automation, or connectors for ELD, maps, EDI, or finance.

  3. Rebuild

    Replace limiting architecture

    When spreadsheets run dispatch, exceptions surface too late, or the legacy system cannot support your model, module-by-module or full platform replacement.

  1. 01

    Map the workflow

    Shadow dispatch, exceptions, and workarounds. Document handoffs, integrations, and what the spreadsheet actually controls.

  2. 02

    Build beside the stack

    Prototype and ship new surfaces or connectors in parallel, feature flags, sanitized staging, weekly demos on a live URL.

  3. 03

    Validate with operators

    Test bulk actions, exception handling, and integrations against real dispatch shapes, not demo data alone.

  4. 04

    Transition on sign-off

    Phased cutover when dispatch approves. Operator docs, data migration checks, and iteration after go-live.

Not sure which path fits? We assess the workflow before recommending the build approach, configuration, integration, modernization, or rebuild.
