One load, end to end

The workflow every screen has to serve.

If a dispatcher cannot move a load through this without opening three tabs, the interface has already failed.

  1. 01
    Load received

    A tender has to be readable in three seconds.

    Lane, weight, pickup window, accessorials — on one row. Decide to take it without leaving the board.

  2. 02
    Assignment

    Assignment is a bulk action, not a per-row chore.

    Multi-select across the board, apply once, audit as a single event. That pattern is what moved handle time on FleetFlow.

  3. 03
    Driver confirmation

    Acceptance happens on a phone, in a yard, on bad signal.

    Load acceptance, status, documents, and a message back to the desk — designed for gloves, glare, and one hand.

  4. 04
    In transit

    Fleet visibility that answers the customer’s question.

    Where it is, whether the window still holds, and who owns the exception if it does not.

  5. 05
    Exception

    The board raises it before the customer calls.

    Detention, breakdown, missed window. Worked by SLA risk, not by who shouted first.

  6. 06
    Supervisor

    Overrides exist. Every one is audited.

    Who changed what, when, and why — recoverable if it was the wrong call.

  7. 07
    Delivered

    POD captured, invoice ready, ledger in sync.

    Delivery closes into accounting. ELD, mapping, and QuickBooks or Xero connected with retries you can actually read.
