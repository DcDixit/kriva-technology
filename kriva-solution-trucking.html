The workflow every screen has to serve.

    Tender & assign

    Readable tender, bulk assignment.

  2. 02
    Driver & transit

    Yard acceptance, fleet visibility.

    Load acceptance on a phone in bad signal. Fleet view answers where it is and whether the window still holds — before the customer calls.

  3. 03
    Exception & override

    Risk on the board, overrides audited.

    Detention, breakdown, missed window — worked by SLA risk, not who shouted first. Every supervisor override logged with who, when, and why.

  4. 04
    Delivery & close

    POD captured, ledger in sync.

    Delivery closes into accounting. ELD, mapping, and QuickBooks or Xero connected with retries and exceptions you can actually read.
