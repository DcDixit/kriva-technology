The close

Month-end should not start in a spreadsheet.

The people who close the books need to see what synced, what did not, and what to do about it. We build that view into the integration, so finance can finish the close without waiting on an engineer.

At a glance

What a QuickBooks or Xero integration should do

A QuickBooks or Xero integration moves financial records from your product into your accounting ledger automatically: invoices, payments, credit notes, customers, and the tax and account codes behind them. A good one also proves the two sides agree. KRIVA builds the connector on the public Intuit and Xero APIs, a match step that compares amounts, tax, entity, and currency, and an exception queue where finance resolves anything that does not match before close.

  1. 01

    Who it is for

    SaaS and operations platforms that already invoice, collect, or pay out, and need those movements to land cleanly in QuickBooks Online or Xero, including teams with books in more than one country.

  2. 02

    What we build

    The connector, data mapping, matching, retry rules, and the operator screens finance uses when a line does not match. Design and engineering come from the same in-house team.

  3. 03

    What this page is not

    CRM, payment, and other operational connectors are covered under API integrations. No-code Zapier or Make workflows are covered under workflow automation.

Our bar: the integration is not finished when data syncs. It is finished when finance can close the books with confidence.

Platforms

The two ledgers we build for

QuickBooks Online and Xero, through their public APIs. A common setup is QuickBooks for US entities and Xero for UK or Australian ones. When you run both, the two connectors share one operator view.

QuickBooks Online

Intuit API

QuickBooks Online is the usual ledger for US books. We map invoices, payments, customers, items, and tax codes through Intuit's public API, with OAuth 2.0 token refresh, backoff when Intuit throttles requests (HTTP 429), and writes keyed to your source records so a retry cannot post twice.

  • Invoices, payments, credit memos, and bills
  • Customers, vendors, and chart of accounts mapping
  • Multi-entity books with separate tokens per QuickBooks company
  • Change data capture polling to catch what webhooks miss

Xero

Xero API

Xero is common for UK, Australian, and dual-region teams. We build a separate connector against Xero's public API, with the same operator dashboard and exception language as QuickBooks, so finance is not learning two products. Xero limits calls per organization (60 a minute, plus a daily allowance that depends on your app's Xero plan), so we queue and pace work instead of firing it in bursts.

  • Multiple Xero organizations under one set of authorizations, with unified triage
  • VAT and multi-currency lines with readable deltas
  • Webhooks plus If-Modified-Since polling so silent drift cannot hide

Why this exists

When finance stops trusting the sync

The connector worked in staging. Month-end is where it breaks. These are the three failure patterns we design against from the first week.

  1. 01

    Silent drift

    Jobs report success while amounts, tax, or entity mapping quietly diverge. Controllers find the difference in a spreadsheet, not in the product.

    Cost: variances surface outside the product

  2. 02

    Errors finance cannot act on

    Exceptions pile up as raw API errors with no invoice, customer, or amount attached. An outsourced bookkeeper cannot fix them without engineering, so the close slips.

    Cost: close waits on an engineering ticket

  3. 03

    Retries that post twice

    A request times out, the job retries, and the same invoice lands in the ledger twice. Without a key tied to the source record, every retry can make the books worse.

    Cost: duplicate postings and overstated balances

  1. 01 Import

    Source events land as records, not a JSON dump.

    Invoices, payments, and credit notes queue with stable keys and idempotent workers. Finance sees counts, not a log file.

  2. 02 Validate

    Schema and tax codes before the match.

    Missing accounts, unknown tax, or a currency the org does not hold fail here, not inside the ledger.

  3. 03 Match

    247 of 248 is a result, not a silent 100%.

    The job reports what matched. The one that did not stays visible instead of disappearing into "success."

  4. 04 Exception

    A variance with a human-readable delta.

    Source 1,204.75 against ledger 1,240.75. Transposition suspected. No raw Intuit or Xero payload in the operator view.

  5. 05 Review

    Finance acts without an engineering ticket.

    Retry, confirm the correct value, or park it. Every action is attributable. Outsourced controllers can finish this step.

  6. 06 Reconcile

    The line closes. The amount is honest.

    Once confirmed, the record matches both books. Close does not wait on a Slack thread.

  7. 07 Audit

    Who changed what, when, and why.

    The trail is written in language an accountant will accept. That is the bar: the integration is finished when finance can confidently close the books.

Architecture

A path from source event to a closed ledger

The same job, seen by your engineering team. Six components we design and build, so each failure surfaces at the stage it belongs to instead of as a generic error at month-end.

  1. 01Source events

    Invoices, payments, payouts, and credit notes from your product or ops platform.

  2. 02Queue & workers

    Idempotent jobs, backoff, and canaries. Retries that cannot duplicate a posting.

  3. 03QBO / Xero API

    Direct calls to the public APIs, token refresh, and rate-limit handling per company or organization.

  4. 04Match engine

    Amount, tax, entity, and currency compared with a readable delta.

  5. 05Exception queue

    Operator triage with retry, confirm, or park, before close rather than after.

  6. 06Ledger + audit

    Closed records and an attributable trail finance can keep.

FAQ

About QuickBooks & Xero integrations

Asked most often by finance and engineering leads before a first call.

QuickBooks Desktop or QuickBooks Online?

We build against QuickBooks Online through Intuit's public API. QuickBooks Desktop uses a different integration route and is not what this page covers; if you are on Desktop, tell us on the fit call and we will say plainly whether we can help. Xero is a cloud API only.

Can you sync both Xero and QuickBooks for different regions?

Yes. Multi-region billing with separate connectors and a unified operator dashboard is a common SaaS pattern: QBO for US entities, Xero for UK, one exception queue.

How do you handle sync failures?

Every job logs errors, retries with backoff and source-record keys so a retry cannot double-post, and surfaces exceptions in an operator dashboard with a next step. Expired or revoked authorizations show up as a reconnect task, not a silent stall. Failures are visible before close, not after.

How is this different from your API integrations service?

API integrations covers CRM, payments, ELD, and general connectors. This page is the finance-specific solution: QuickBooks, Xero, matching, the close, and the operator screens around exceptions. Many products need both.

How long does a typical integration take?

A focused QBO or Xero connector with operator UX usually takes weeks, not days: mapping sign-off, a build against a sandbox, and a parallel run on live books. Production access also depends on Intuit's app assessment for QuickBooks and Xero's connection limits for new apps. Dual-region or multi-entity setups run longer. We give a real range on the first call, not a padded one.

Stripe or payment processor sync?

Yes, as source events. Payments and payouts from a processor such as Stripe enter the same queue as invoices and are matched in the ledger. Building the payment connector itself is covered under API integrations; the matching and close screens are covered here.

What data can a QuickBooks or Xero integration sync?

Typically invoices, payments, credit notes, customers or contacts, and the account, item, and tax codes they depend on. In QuickBooks Online that can include bills and vendors; in Xero, bank transactions and tracking categories. The exact list, and which system is the source of truth for each record, is agreed and signed off during data mapping.

How is pricing structured?

Fixed-scope projects, monthly retainers, or sprint-based work. Everything is scoped after discovery, with deliverables and milestones agreed upfront, so you know the cost before the build starts. You own the code and documentation at handover.
