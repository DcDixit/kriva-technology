SEO and digital marketing services: search, paid media, social, and reporting

Who it's for

Founders and ops leads who need demand they can defend in a board pack. Teams where organic, paid, and social each have a vendor — but nobody can show the same keyword map or explain why spend moved.

What we deliver

Technical SEO, local and on-page search, Google and Meta ads, social systems, and performance marketing — with GA4, Search Console, and ad accounts you can open yourself. Creative and landing pages when the site is the bottleneck.

Problems we solve

Broken crawl and index, conversion events that fire twice or never, and monthly PDFs with no next action. We fix the pipes first, then content and campaigns — on a shared keyword map so paid and organic do not fight.

Kriva is a product design and engineering studio — not a ranking-guarantee shop. You keep account access, documentation, and the readout after the engagement.

Where it breaks

When marketing cannot
show the work.

Search, ads, and reporting fail in predictable ways. We name the failure mode before we scale spend or publish another landing page.

01

The site cannot be crawled cleanly

Duplicate URLs, missing canonicals, slow templates. Content cannot outrun a broken index.COST: pages never rank, paid sends traffic to the wrong URL

02

Spend without an event you trust

Pixels fire on the thank-you page twice, or never. ROAS is a slide, not a number in the account.COST: budget scaled on fiction, CRM and ads disagree

03

Vanity charts, no next action

Sessions up, pipeline unchanged. The monthly PDF does not tell ops what to ship next.COST: meetings about charts, not about pages or campaigns

Organic and paid on the same keyword map. Creative that matches the landing page. Reports a founder can read.

Method

Hygiene, then spend.

Fix crawl and conversion events first. Then content and campaigns. Then scale what the readout supports.

  1. 01

    Baseline

    Search Console, ads accounts, analytics. What is indexed, what is tracked, what is a guess.

  2. 02

    Fix the pipes

    Technical SEO, canonicals, sitemap, and conversion events. No scale until this holds.

  3. 03

    Run & report

    On-page, local, and paid on a shared map. A monthly readout with the next three actions named.
