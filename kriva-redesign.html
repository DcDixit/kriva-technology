Build, integrate, or keep the current system

Custom software is useful only when the workflow is the advantage.

Keep or configure the current system when

  • Standard workflows fit the operation
  • Integrations are stable
  • Workarounds are minor
  • User adoption is acceptable
  • The current system can be extended safely

Consider customization or a new product when

  • Spreadsheets run core operational decisions
  • High-volume actions require repeated manual work
  • Exceptions are discovered too late
  • Unique settlements or integrations are business-critical
  • Existing roles and permissions do not match the operation
  • The product prevents growth or adoption

PayrollPro · SaaS

SSO was live. Activation was not.

Problem. Admins landed in empty workspaces. End users bounced between docs and support.

Decision. Role paths in parallel, permission copy that names who can grant access, first-run tied to a real payroll cycle.

Shipped. Progressive onboarding and permission clarity after SSO. No signed-off metrics on this case.

FinanceSync · Integrations

Silent drift, then finance stopped trusting the sync.

Problem. QuickBooks and Xero drifted across entities; controllers lost trust before close.

Decision. Idempotent workers, discrepancy cards with remediation steps, no raw JSON in the operator view.

Shipped. Reconciliation hub with retry history. Measured metrics are not published until signed off.
