Ten weeks, three approval gates

You always know what you're approving next.

Full process overview →

Discovery

Week 1–2 You sign off

Goals, users, constraints, and a written definition of success. Nothing gets designed until you've read it back to us.

UX & UI

Week 2–4

Wireframes, a clickable prototype, then final UI — reviewed with you before a line of production code exists.

Build

Week 4–8 You sign off

Weekly working demos on a real URL. Not a slide about progress — the thing itself, in your browser.

QA & launch

Week 8–10

Cross-browser, accessibility, analytics, phased rollout, and documentation your team can actually maintain.

After

Week 10+ You sign off

Fixes, iteration, roadmap support. Most clients keep going — the ones who don't leave with everything they need.
