Questions

What buyers ask us first.

See all questions →
What does KRIVA build, and for whom?

We design and build custom software for trucking companies, freight brokers, SaaS teams, and finance operations: dispatch CRM and TMS, fleet dashboards, driver apps, SaaS products, and QuickBooks and Xero integrations.

Should we buy a TMS instead of building one?

Often, yes. If your freight and workflows are standard, a boxed TMS will be cheaper and faster, and we will say so. Custom pays off when spreadsheets run your core decisions or your workflow is your edge. Our build vs buy guide covers how to decide.

How long does a project take?

Discovery takes 1 to 2 weeks. A focused MVP or redesign is usually 6 to 10 weeks, and a full platform with integrations usually runs 12 to 20 weeks. We give you an honest timeline on the first call.

How is pricing structured?

Fixed-scope projects, sprint-based work, or a monthly retainer. Everything is scoped after discovery, with deliverables and milestones agreed up front.

Can you change our system without stopping operations?

Yes. New screens run beside your current system behind feature flags, tested in staging and reviewed weekly, and they go live in phases once you sign off.

How do you work with US teams from India?

Our team is in Ahmedabad and overlaps with US and UK hours for calls and demos, with written updates in between. In-person meetings are not required.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Figma files, repositories, and documentation transfer to you at project close or earlier, with no lock-in.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. Mutual NDAs are standard before we discuss sensitive product details or architecture.
