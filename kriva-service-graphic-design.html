Who graphic design is for, what we deliver, and what it is not

Who it’s for

Marketing leads and founders at carriers, brokers, freight-tech vendors, and SaaS companies whose decks, rate sheets, and social posts have drifted from the brand. Teams that want a kit, not a one-time file.

What we deliver

Sales and investor decks, capability one-pagers, trade-show and print graphics, email and social templates, and workflow diagrams, as editable source files with a short use guide.

What it isn’t

An identity from scratch (that is branding), a monthly production desk, or print management. We build the kit; your team produces the day-to-day pieces from it.

KRIVA is a product design and engineering studio. Graphic design is a supporting service, usually paired with branding or a product launch. You own the source files and templates after delivery.

Where it breaks

When collateral is rebuilt every quarter.

The deck, the one-pager, and the product should read as one company. Without a kit, each one starts on a blank slide.

01

The deck is a different company than the product

Slides use a default theme and old screenshots, while the product has since changed.IMPACT: the pitch and the demo disagree

02

Workflow diagrams are redrawn by whoever has time

How a load moves from quote to delivery, or how an integration syncs, looks different on every slide.IMPACT: inconsistent, and slow to update

03

Nobody can open the source

Files exist only as PDFs or flattened images from a former vendor.IMPACT: every edit means starting over

How we run it

Audit, kit, handoff.

We start from your brand rules, or from a branding engagement if they do not exist yet.

  1. 01 · Audit

    Audit what exists

    We collect logo files, past decks, screenshots, and brand rules. You provide: source files, current product access for screenshots, and the person who approves.

  2. 02 · Kit

    Build the kit

    Layouts, grid, and components for each format, tested with your real content rather than placeholder text.

  3. 03 · Handoff

    Hand off

    Editable source files, export presets, and a one-page use guide, so your team can make the next piece without us.
