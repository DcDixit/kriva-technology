Graphic design

Print-ready · Social templates · Brand system

Graphic design services — corporate identity, print, social, packaging, and marketing systems

Who it's for

Marketing teams, founders, and ops-led brands where the flyer, LinkedIn header, and booth look like three different companies. Teams that need a kit they can reuse — not a new artboard every campaign.

What we deliver

Corporate identity, logo lockups, print and stationery, social templates, packaging, presentations, and campaign kits — one art direction with print specs, social crops, and a short use guide in the same file set.

Problems we solve

One-off assets, print and product that diverge, and files nobody else can open. We lock type, color, and layout first, then produce the surfaces you actually ship this quarter.

Kriva is a product design and engineering studio — not a clip-art vendor. You own the source files, brand rules, and templates after delivery.

Where it breaks

When every asset
is a one-off.

The booth, the feed, and the deck should read as one company. Without a system, every campaign starts from a blank artboard.

01

Looks like anyone in the category

The flyer, the LinkedIn header, and the booth could belong to any firm on the pitch list.IMPACT: forgettable brand, weak recall at events and in feeds

02

Print and product diverge

The website, the deck, and the packaging do not look like one company.IMPACT: trust drops when touchpoints disagree

03

No file anyone else can use

No templates, no print specs, no social grid — so every campaign starts from scratch.IMPACT: slow production, off-brand edits, vendor lock-in
