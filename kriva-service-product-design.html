Challenges

When screens ship without a product story

The vision is in a deck. The UI is in Figma. Nobody can walk the product. These are the patterns we see before an umbrella engagement starts.

01

Vision without UI

The product story exists in slides, but UI execution lags. Engineering starts guessing, and the first build does not match what leadership thought they approved.

02

Priority fog

Stakeholders disagree on what ships first because there is no shared prototype. Every meeting re-litigates the same journeys.

03

Leftover surfaces

Dashboards and settings ship last, or never. The happy path looks finished; the product people actually live in still feels unfinished.

FAQ

About Product Design

Is this different from UI/UX design?

Yes. Product Design covers strategy, flows, and launch-ready UI for the full product, including settings and dashboards. UI/UX Design is narrower: research-led flows, prototypes, and interface systems for screens and journeys as the product grows. Choose this page when you need the whole narrative, not a surface.

How is this different from SaaS Product Design?

SaaS Product Design is for live multi-tenant products: workspace context, permissions, and admin versus customer surfaces. Product Design is the umbrella when the product story itself is not yet agreed. A live SaaS rebuild often starts here, then narrows into SaaS Product Design.

Are settings and dashboards in scope?

Yes. The brief includes leftover surfaces by default. If the need is only a morning-scan analytics view, that work lives under Dashboard Design. If it is the whole product including those views, it stays here.

Can research and prototyping sit inside this engagement?

Yes. This is often the umbrella. User research, wireframes, and interactive prototypes nest inside when the brief is end-to-end. If you only need flows and handoff into a live product, start on UI/UX Design.
