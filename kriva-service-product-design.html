FAQ

About Product Design

Is this different from UI/UX design?

Yes. Product Design owns the whole-product story. UI/UX Design owns feature-level flows and handoff. Research and prototyping can nest inside this engagement.

How is this different from SaaS Product Design?

SaaS Product Design owns tenant-aware UX, permissions, and admin versus customer surfaces on a live product. Start there if the product already has workspaces and roles.

Are settings and dashboards in scope?

Yes. Leftover surfaces are the point. A product story that skips them is a pitch, not a shippable product.

Can research and prototyping sit inside this engagement?

Yes. They nest here when the brief is the whole product. They can also run as their own services when the brief is narrower.
