Product design

One product story before code.

Senior product design for SaaS and digital products — strategy through Figma prototypes and launch-ready UI, before engineering starts.

Figma-first · Engineering-ready handoff

Product design services for SaaS and digital products — strategy to launch-ready UI

Who it's for

Founders, product teams, and engineering leads shipping SaaS or digital products. Teams where the pitch deck looks finished but settings, invites, and empty states were never designed — or stakeholders argue every sprint because nobody has clicked the same path.

What we deliver

End-to-end product design: strategy, user journeys, clickable prototypes, and launch-ready UI with engineering-ready handoff. One complete product story — core flows, edge cases, permissions, and the operational surfaces that usually get skipped.

Problems we solve

Hero-screen design, misaligned stakeholders, and UI that arrives after the story is frozen. We map the whole product first, then design the surfaces engineering can build against — not a six-step agency deck.

Kriva is a product design and engineering studio — not a design-only agency. You own the designs, prototypes, and documentation after launch.
