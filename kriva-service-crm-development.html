Two desks, one discipline

Dispatch and sales are not the same board.

Stages, bulk work, and exceptions are the shared language. The mistake is forcing a sales template onto a load board.

01 · Ops floor

Dispatch CRM & TMS

Load boards for US carriers, brokers, and fleets. Assignment is a bulk action. SLA sits on the row. Supervisor overrides are audited.

  • Multi-select assign as a single event
  • Exception and detention visible before the customer calls
  • Role density for desk and tablet

02 · Revenue desk

Sales pipeline

HubSpot or Salesforce stages with exit criteria the team actually uses. Forecast views that match the floor, not a leadership deck.

  • Stage model with exit criteria
  • Role layouts for rep and manager
  • Rules with rollback, not silent automation

What fails

When the CRM becomes a spreadsheet again.

01 · Board

Work happens beside the tool

Bulk assign, SLA, and exceptions live in a sheet. The CRM is a system of record nobody updates until Friday.

02 · Stages

Stages without exit criteria

Anyone can drag a deal or a load forward. Reporting looks healthy. The floor does not trust it.

03 · Roles

One layout for every job

Supervisor, dispatcher, and rep see the same density. Overrides are unaudited. Exceptions hide in filters.

Method

Design around how the team already works.

  1. 01 · Shadow

    Watch the desk

    We sit with the board people actually use — including the spreadsheet beside it — before we draw a stage model.

  2. 02 · Stages

    Exit criteria, then UX

    Stages, bulk actions, and exception paths are designed as events the floor can complete in one motion.

  3. 03 · Console

    HubSpot, Salesforce, or custom

    Platform UX when that is enough. A custom console when the desk outgrew the template.

FAQ

About CRM Design

Which CRM platforms do you design for?

HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and custom CRM or TMS consoles. We design for the tool you run today, or the console you are building next.

Is this dispatch CRM, sales CRM, or both?

Both. Dispatch boards are a primary KRIVA path. Sales pipelines use the same stage discipline on HubSpot or Salesforce. We do not force a sales template onto a load board.

How is this different from Dashboard Design?

CRM Design owns stage models, pipeline UX, and desk workflows. Dashboard Design owns widget hierarchy, filters, and drill-downs. Many engagements use both.

Do you rebuild the CRM or improve the one we have?

Either. Platform UX improvements stay inside HubSpot or Salesforce when that is enough. Custom consoles when the desk outgrew the template.
