CRM problems

The real workflow should not live beside the CRM.

When the product does not support bulk actions or role density, the desk builds workarounds in spreadsheets and checklists.

01

Spreadsheet side systems

High-volume actions live outside the CRM. The desk runs parallel systems: system of record and system of work.

02

Repetitive row-by-row actions

Every record change is one manual update. Bulk operations force workarounds. Update cycles slow to hours or days.

03

Unclear ownership or SLA

Stage models lack exit criteria. Ownership is ambiguous. SLA or risk is hidden in a separate report the team never opens.

Two paths, one discipline

Different teams need different CRM behavior.

Operational CRM and sales CRM solve different workflows. They share stage discipline, bulk work, and clear exceptions. The interface is shaped to fit.

Operational CRM · Dispatch

Loads, drivers, schedules, and exceptions

Real-time assignment consoles for US trucking, freight, and logistics. Bulk actions apply one. SLA sits on every row. Supervisor overrides are audited.

  • Bulk selection and actions
  • SLA and ownership visibility
  • Dispatcher and supervisor views
  • Audited overrides
  • Fast exception recovery

Sales CRM · Revenue

Leads, accounts, and opportunities

HubSpot or Salesforce stages with exit criteria the team actually enforces. Rep and manager layouts. Rules with rollback, not silent automation.

  • Clear stage exit criteria
  • Rep and manager views
  • Required information before progression
  • Automation with rollback
  • Pipeline and forecast visibility

The high-volume action

One action should not require five separate updates.

  1. 01
    Select

    Multiple records. Risk and SLA stay visible as they are selected.

  2. 02
    Validate

    Check permissions, conflicts, and required fields before applying the action.

  3. 03
    Apply

    One bulk action applied to all selected records. Confirm once.

  4. 04
    Confirm and audit

    Record written to the audit trail: who, when, affected records, and outcome.

The right approach

Use the current CRM until the workflow proves it needs more.

Not every workflow needs a custom build. Operational fitness is the test.

Option 1

Keep or Configure

Use HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive when standard workflows and platform configuration are sufficient.

  • Stage model in place
  • Bulk actions are native
  • Role-based views work
  • Data is current
Option 2

Extend or Integrate

Add focused interfaces, automation, APIs, or accounting integrations when the current CRM should remain the source of record.

  • One workflow needs redesign
  • Platform cannot handle bulk work
  • Integrations are weak
  • Customization is limited
Option 3

Build Custom

Create an operational CRM when bulk work, role density, exception handling, permissions, or business-specific workflows cannot fit the existing platform.

  • Spreadsheets run core work
  • Exceptions are invisible
  • Platform cannot scale
  • Overrides need audit

Integrations and automation

The CRM must work with the systems around it.

CRM design owns the desk workflow. Integration design ensures the CRM talks to QuickBooks, Xero, internal tools, and operational systems with visible errors and human approval where required.

Reliability in practice

  • Visible failed syncs in the CRM
  • Retry queues and reconciliation states
  • Clear validation errors and conflicts
  • Audited changes and overrides
  • Human approval where required
  • No silent automation

Delivery process

Map the workflow before changing the system.

Four stages from discovery to handoff. One team throughout. No subcontracting.

01

Map and research

Understand users, roles, handoffs, current workarounds, and constraints. Document real workflows, not ideal ones.

02

Prototype and validate

Build realistic workflows with edge cases. Iterate with the desk until the model holds the work.

03

Build and integrate

Production-ready code, integrations, and admin tooling. Weekly demos and clear gates between phases.

04

Roll out safely and transfer

Pilot or run in parallel. Train the team. Transfer code, Figma files, and documentation to your accounts. Ready for retainer or handoff.
