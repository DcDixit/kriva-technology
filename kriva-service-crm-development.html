Challenges

When the CRM becomes a spreadsheet again

The product is live. Adoption is not. These are the patterns we see before a redesign starts.

01

Pipeline distrust

Stages mean different things to sales, ops, and finance. Forecast meetings become archaeology. People keep a private spreadsheet “just to be sure.”

02

Export dependency

Reports that should be a view become CSV dumps, pivot tables, and Slack threads. Leadership stops opening the CRM for anything but status theater.

03

UI / process mismatch

Default templates force clicks the desk never makes. Bulk actions, overrides, and exceptions live outside the tool, so the board never matches reality.

Approach

Design the CRM around how the team already works

Stage definitions first, then console UX, then automation rules, reviewed before engineering commits.

01

Stage reality workshop

We sit with the people who move deals and loads. Exit criteria, owners, and handoffs get written so the board stops arguing with the floor.

You leave withA stage model the desk can defend in a standup

02

Console & pipeline UX

Boards, list views, and role layouts for the people who open the tool every day. Bulk actions, risk signals, and supervisor overrides where the desk needs them.

03

Automation map

Rules for assignment, reminders, and stage moves, with rollback paths. Nothing silent writes into production without an operator path back.

You leave withAn automation map ops will actually trust

FAQ

About CRM Design

Which CRM platforms do you design for?

HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and custom CRM builds. We design for the tool you use or the console you are building.

How is this different from Dashboard Design?

CRM Design owns stage models, pipeline UX, and desk workflows. Dashboard Design owns widget hierarchy, filters, and drill-downs for analytics views. Many engagements use both.

Do you rebuild the CRM or improve the one we have?

Either. Platform UX improvements stay inside HubSpot or Salesforce when that is enough. Custom consoles when the desk outgrew the template and the product is the CRM itself.

What do we need before a kickoff?

Access to a few operators for a stage workshop, a walkthrough of the current board, and clarity on who owns forecast truth. An NDA is available before the first call.
