Why generic CRM still fails

Three ways the board loses the floor.

01

Work happens beside the tool

The high-volume action is not a first-class control, so the desk builds a second system.

02

Stages have no exit criteria

Anyone can drag a deal or a load forward. Reporting looks healthy. Nobody trusts it.

03

One layout serves every role

Dispatchers, reps, and supervisors receive the same density. Overrides go unaudited.
