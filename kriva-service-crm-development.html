A dispatch CRM that retires the side spreadsheet

Custom CRM and ops consoles for trucking and logistics teams: load boards with bulk assignment, SLA on every row, exception queues, and audited supervisor overrides. We also shape HubSpot-style CRMs for B2B sales and service teams.

Design and build · Runs beside your TMS

Who a custom dispatch or ops CRM is for, what we deliver, and where it stops

Who it's for

Dispatch managers, ops leads, and brokerage teams at US carriers and logistics companies whose TMS holds the load but a spreadsheet runs the shift. Also B2B sales and service teams whose CRM records the deal but not the work.

What we deliver

A workflow map, role-based views, bulk actions, exception queues, and an audit trail. Built as a console beside your TMS or CRM, or as a configured workflow layer in HubSpot-style tools, whichever the evidence supports.

Where it stops

We are a design and engineering studio, not a CRM or TMS vendor. Your TMS stays the system of record unless you choose otherwise. We do not promise a rebuild when configuration would do.

Designs, code, and documentation transfer to your accounts after launch.

Where the desk works around the system

The real dispatch work should not live beside the TMS.

When bulk actions and role-based views are missing, the desk builds workarounds. Each one has a cost.

01

A spreadsheet beside the TMS

Assignment, appointment times, and SLA tracking live in a shared sheet because the system of record cannot show them together.COST: double entry, no audit trail, stale system data

02

One load, one click, forty times

Reassigning a lane after a breakdown or weather delay means editing loads one at a time.COST: dispatch backlog, supervisor rework

03

Nobody owns the late load

Exceptions such as detention, a missed appointment, or a missing POD sit in a report nobody opens until the customer calls.COST: exceptions surface late, customers call first

What we build

Different desks. One load record.

Everyone works from the same record. What changes by role is density, fields, and the actions within reach.

Dispatch desk

Load board with bulk assignment and SLA

Multi-select, one approval, one audit entry. Exceptions sorted by time remaining, with owner and next action on the row.

Field supervisors

Thumb-friendly actions on a tablet or phone

The desk needs density. A supervisor in the yard needs a few large actions. Same record, different layout, audited overrides.

Billing and settlement

Detention, POD holds, and invoice readiness

See which delivered loads are held up by a missing document or an unapproved accessorial, and what to send to QuickBooks or Xero once they clear.

Sales and account teams

Pipeline stages that match how you sell

Stage definitions with exit criteria, rep and manager views, and forecast screens, as in the CRMPulse HubSpot work. Useful for brokers, carriers, and B2B software teams alike.

How we work

Use the current system until the workflow proves it needs more.

Not every desk needs a custom build. We look at the work first and recommend the smallest change that holds.

Three ways to engage

Configure

Tune the CRM you have

Fields, views, permissions, and reports in HubSpot or a similar CRM, when its native bulk actions already cover the job.

Extend

Add a console beside it

Keep the TMS or CRM as the record. Add a dispatch board, exception queue, or automation where one workflow is the gap.

Rebuild

Replace a module that blocks the work

When spreadsheets carry core work and the platform cannot support bulk actions or audited overrides, we replace one module at a time.

Delivery process

Map the workflow before changing the system.

Six steps, from watching the desk to a phased rollout.

  1. 01 · Map

    Watch the desk

    Shadow dispatchers and supervisors. Write down handoffs, workarounds, and what the system is supposed to control.

  2. 02 · Friction

    Find the friction

    Locate where side sheets, missing permissions, or missing bulk actions push work outside the system.

  3. 03 · Design

    Prototype with the desk

    Role-based views, bulk actions, and exception handling tested with the people who will use them.

  4. 04 · Build

    Build and connect

    Console, connectors, and automation beside the existing stack, shown regularly on a live URL.

  5. 05 · Validate

    Test with operators

    Bulk actions and edge cases run against sanitized data shaped like yours, not only demo records.

  6. 06 · Roll out

    Phase it in

    Cutover by role or module. Code, designs, and documentation move to your accounts.

Not sure which path fits? Send the workflow and the system it lives in. We will tell you honestly whether it needs a build.
