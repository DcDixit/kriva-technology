The problem

The site is doing two jobs, and most templates do neither

A shipper or broker wants equipment, coverage, authority, and a quote path. A driver wants pay, home time, equipment, and a way to apply. A single “About us / Contact” page makes the office answer questions the site should have answered.

FAQ

Questions we hear before a quote

Do you only design the site, or do you build it too?

Both. The starting website scope includes design and development, a mobile layout, one quote or recruiting form, and launch on your domain. A customer portal or driver app is a separate project.

Can the site collect a freight quote?

Yes. A useful trucking form asks for origin, destination, equipment, dates, and contact details, instead of a single message box. Live pricing from a TMS is custom work and is quoted separately.

We already have a TMS. Do we still need a website?

Usually yes. Shippers, brokers, and drivers decide whether to call before they ever see the TMS. The public site is that first conversation. The dispatch software is what the desk uses after the load exists.
