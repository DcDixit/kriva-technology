Operating conditions

What makes German logistics and SaaS builds different.

German products meet tax, data-protection, and transport rules early, often at the first enterprise customer or the first month-end with the Steuerberater. Designing for them from the start is cheaper than retrofitting. We build the screens and data model that record these obligations; we do not give legal or tax advice, and your compliance owner signs off the rules.

  • E-invoicing: XRechnung and ZUGFeRD (EN 16931) for B2B invoices, which German businesses have had to be able to receive since January 2025
  • GoBD-compliant records and DATEV-ready exports, so month-end does not mean retyping
  • GDPR (DSGVO): data export, deletion, audit trails, and an EU hosting region in the admin panel
  • EU driving and rest times (Regulation 561/2006) and tachograph data for fleets
  • Lkw-Maut and cross-border EU loads on one dispatch desk

Tax

E-invoices and DATEV-ready exports

Since January 2025 every German business has had to be able to receive structured e-invoices, and issuing them becomes mandatory in stages from 2027. When your product creates invoices, we generate XRechnung or ZUGFeRD files from the same records and export bookings in formats your tax adviser’s DATEV setup can import, so the figures in the accounts trace back to source records.

Data protection

DSGVO in the admin panel

German enterprise buyers and works councils ask early where data is hosted, how it is exported, corrected, and deleted, and who saw it. We build these as real admin features: an EU hosting region, role-based access, an audit log, and export and deletion flows. They answer a vendor questionnaire with screenshots rather than promises.

Transport

Driving and rest times before the tour is assigned

EU rules cap driving at 4.5 hours before a 45-minute break and limit daily and weekly driving time, all recorded on the tachograph. Dispatch software that ignores this lets a desk plan work a driver cannot legally do. We bring hours and vehicle data into the planning view, so the conflict appears before the tour is assigned, with toll-relevant routes visible on the same board.
