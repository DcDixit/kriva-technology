Checklist

What the site should do

  • 01Separate local, long-distance, packing, and storage if you sell them
  • 02Name the cities or corridors you actually serve
  • 03Ask for origin, destination, home or office size, date, and access details
  • 04Offer a phone number that is easy to tap
  • 05Give crews a page if you are hiring
  • 06Work on a phone, because that is where the search started

FAQ

Questions we hear before a quote

Will the site give customers an instant moving price?

Only if your pricing rules are simple enough to write down. Many movers should collect origin, destination, move size, date, and stairs or elevator, then confirm the price. We will say which approach fits before we build it.

Can you build a booking app or a moving CRM?

Yes, as custom software, quoted separately. The published moving work on this site is website design. We will not pretend a booking product already exists in the portfolio.

Which US cities do you serve?

We work remotely with moving companies across the United States. We do not create a separate page for each state. The site should describe the cities and corridors you actually serve.
