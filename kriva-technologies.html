Technology stack for custom trucking software, SaaS platforms, and integrations

Who it's for

Product owners, ops leaders, and founders scoping custom software — trucking dispatch, multi-tenant SaaS, finance sync, or internal ops tools.

What this stack builds

Dispatch consoles, driver apps, admin dashboards, customer portals, API connectors, and automation — production surfaces your team runs daily, not a logo directory.

How we choose

Match the tool to the workflow and your team's capacity. Custom when architecture matters; no-code when validation speed wins — same human oversight either way.

Kriva is a product design and engineering studio in Ahmedabad. You own the code, designs, and documentation after launch. See About for how we work with US, UK, UAE, and Canada teams.
