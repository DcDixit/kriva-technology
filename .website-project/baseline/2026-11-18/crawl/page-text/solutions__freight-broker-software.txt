AT A GLANCE What is custom freight broker software? Freight broker software is the system a brokerage uses to quote shipper freight, find and book carriers, track loads in transit, collect documents, bill customers, and pay carriers. A custom build shapes those screens around how your desk actually works, instead of the workflow a packaged TMS assumes. WHO IT'S FOR US freight brokerages and 3PLs whose quoting, carrier sales, or customer service has outgrown spreadsheets and inbox workarounds. WHAT WE BUILD Quoting and customer tools, carrier sales and onboarding, a load tracking board, documents and billing, and margin reporting. HOW IT FITS Usually beside the TMS you already run, connected through its API or data export. Full replacement only when it clearly pays off. WHO BUILDS IT KRIVA, a product design and engineering studio. You own the code, designs, and documentation. THE PROBLEM Where broker desks lose time and margin 01 Quotes live in email Rates come from memory, a spreadsheet, and a load board. Nobody can see which quotes won, on which lanes, or why. 02 Carrier sales is a phone list Your best carriers sit in one broker's notes. When that broker is out, the relationship goes with them. 03 Check calls eat the afternoon Status lives in texts and calls, so customers ask where their freight is before your team knows. 04 Carrier checks happen late Authority and insurance get reviewed in a rush, after the load is already promised to the shipper. 05 Paperwork holds up cash Rate confirmations, proof of delivery, and invoices sit in different places, so billing waits on someone finding a PDF. 06 Margin shows up at month-end You find out which customers and lanes made money long after you could have acted on it. HOW THE SOFTWARE FOLLOWS A LOAD One load record, from quote to carrier pay Each step below writes to the same load, so the next person never has to ask what happened. Your build may cover all of them or start with the two that hurt most. 01 Quote Price the lane from your own quote history and the rate sources you already use. Won and lost quotes are logged by customer and lane. 02 Book An accepted quote or a customer tender becomes a load, including tenders that arrive by EDI where the shipper sends them. 03 Cover Offer the load to carriers who have run the lane before. Every call, offer, and counter-rate is recorded on the load. 04 Vet Authority, insurance, and your own carrier rules are checked before the rate confirmation goes out, not after. 05 Track Check calls and tracking updates land on the load. Late pickups and missed appointments rise to the top of the board. 06 Deliver Proof of delivery is attached to the load, along with accessorials such as detention or lumper fees. 07 Bill and pay The customer invoice and the carrier payable come from the same record, then sync to QuickBooks or Xero. Buy a TMS if your freight and process are fairly standard. A good off-the-shelf product is cheaper and quicker. Build when your edge is how you quote, cover, or serve a niche, and the software keeps forcing workarounds. Many brokerages land in the middle: keep the TMS as the record, and build the desk tools around it.
