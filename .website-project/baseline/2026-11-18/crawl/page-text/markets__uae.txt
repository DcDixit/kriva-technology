SaaS solutions OPERATING CONDITIONS What makes UAE builds different. Most logistics and SaaS software assumes a single country, one currency, one language, and one tax regime. UAE operators rarely work that way, and the gap usually shows up as spreadsheets sitting beside the system rather than inside it. 01 Mainland and free-zone entities under one operator view 02 AED alongside USD and other settlement currencies 03 Arabic and English interfaces, including right-to-left layout 04 Cross-border GCC movements with customs documentation 05 VAT treatment and e-invoicing requirements as they phase in CROSS-BORDER Cross-border and customs-aware dispatch A load moving Dubai to Riyadh is not the same object as a load moving across one emirate. It carries border wait time, customs paperwork, and a driver who may be out of coverage for hours. Dispatch consoles that ignore this show a job as simply late. We model the checkpoint states explicitly so the desk can tell a customs hold apart from a driver problem and escalate the right one. Straight answers about working with a remote studio from the UAE. If something here does not match your situation, say so in the brief and we will tell you honestly whether we are a fit. 01 Do you have an office in the UAE? No. KRIVA is remote-first from Ahmedabad, India, and we do not claim a local presence we do not have. Gulf Standard Time is two and a half hours ahead of us, so a normal UAE working day overlaps almost entirely with ours - in practice that means same-day replies and live sessions at ordinary hours for both sides. 02 Can you build Arabic and English in the same product? Yes, and it is much cheaper to plan for at the start. We structure layouts, components, and content for bidirectional text from the wireframe stage. Translation itself is usually handled by your team or a translation partner; what we own is making sure the interface does not break when the direction flips. 03 Which accounting systems can you integrate with? QuickBooks and Xero are the two we work with most, using their official APIs. For regional ERPs and bank feeds we work against whatever documented API exists, and we say plainly when a system has no viable integration path rather than building a fragile scraper. See accounting integrations for how the reconciliation workflow is built. 04 Who owns the code and the designs? You do. Repositories, Figma files, and documentation transfer to you at close or earlier if you prefer. There is no licensing arrangement and no lock-in to us for future work. 05 Can you improve an existing system instead of rebuilding it? Usually, and it is normally the better option. We assess what your current platform can actually support, then build beside it in phases behind feature flags so the desk keeps running while the work lands. A full rebuild only makes sense when the platform genuinely cannot carry the workload. Our delivery process sets out how the phasing works. MARKETS WE SERVE Five markets, one in-house team.
