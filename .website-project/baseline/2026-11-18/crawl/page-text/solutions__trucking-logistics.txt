KRIVA designs and builds custom trucking software for US carriers and brokers: dispatch consoles, driver and carrier apps, operations dashboards, and integrations with your TMS, ELD or telematics provider, EDI partners, and QuickBooks or Xero. Most projects extend the TMS you already run rather than replace it. 01 Who it's for Carriers, brokers, and operations teams whose dispatch desk works differently than a generic TMS assumes. 02 What we deliver Dispatch consoles, driver and carrier interfaces, operations views, and integrations, built around your workflow, whether that extends your current TMS or replaces one limiting piece. 03 Problems we solve Spreadsheet queues, manual status updates, and late exceptions. We map how loads move today, then build the screen or connector that closes the gap. KRIVA is a product design and engineering studio, not a boxed TMS vendor. Your team still runs the operation. When the record and the shift drift apart, dispatch falls back on spreadsheets, calls, and chat. Not because every TMS fails, but because the day-to-day path was never captured in software. Our dispatch CRM guide for US trucking companies covers what works when spreadsheets still run the shift. 01 Spreadsheet dispatch queues The real queue lives in a shared sheet dispatch trusts more than the TMS.
