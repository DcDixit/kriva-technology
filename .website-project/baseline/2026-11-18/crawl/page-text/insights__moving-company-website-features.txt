The features that matter are the ones that turn a search into a move request the office can price, not a longer list of animations. NOVEMBER 14, 2026 7 MIN READ KRIVA TECHNOLOGIES Most moving websites fail in the same place. The homepage talks about care and professionalism, and the form asks the customer to “tell us about your move.” The coordinator still has to call and ask where, when, how big, and whether there are stairs. Build the site around that missing conversation. Features worth paying for 1. A request form that matches how you quote Ask for origin, destination, move date, home or office size, and access: stairs, elevator, long carry. A phone number and email are not a substitute for those fields. If you offer local and long-distance moves, the form should know which one the customer thinks they need, even if you correct it later. 2. Service pages that can be booked differently Local moving, long distance, packing, storage, and commercial moves do not share one explanation. Give each service you actually sell its own page, with the next step on that page. Do not add services you hope to sell next year. 3. A service area written in plain cities Name the cities and corridors you cover. “Nationwide” with no terminals is hard for a customer to trust and hard for a search engine to place. KRIVA does not publish a landing page per state. One honest service-area page is more useful than fifty thin city pages. 4. A phone number that works on a mobile screen A large share of moving searches happen on a phone, often the week of a deadline. The number should be tappable, and the form should be usable without pinching. Click-to-call is a feature. A map widget that pushes the form below the fold is not. 5. Proof you can stand behind Use reviews, insurance, and license details you can point to. Do not invent testimonials or star ratings. If you are hiring crews, give them pay, territory, and a way to apply, separate from the customer form. 6. A path into the office system, when you are ready A marketing site does not schedule a crew. When the form volume is the problem, the next product is a job board or a customer booking flow, plus an invoice path to QuickBooks or Xero. That is custom software. Questions we hear before a quote Should a moving site show prices? Show prices only when the rule is stable enough that the number on the site matches the number the coordinator quotes. Otherwise, collect the move details and price it properly. A wrong instant price creates the call you were trying to avoid. How many pages does a mover need? Enough to separate the services you sell. A local mover with packing and storage often needs a homepage, two or three service pages, a service-area page, and contact. Six pages is the scope inside KRIVA’s $4,800 starting price.
