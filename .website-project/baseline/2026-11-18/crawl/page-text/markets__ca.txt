SaaS solutions OPERATING CONDITIONS What makes Canadian builds different. Canadian operators deal with provincial variation and cross-border US traffic that a US-built product rarely handles well. The friction shows up in tax lines, language, and border paperwork. We build the screens and data model that record these obligations; we do not give legal or tax advice, and your compliance owner signs off the rules. A Toronto–Chicago load needs an eManifest filed before the truck reaches the border, and a customs hold is not the same problem as a late driver. We track border and paperwork status as explicit states on the load, so the desk can tell what is actually blocking a delivery and chase the right party. SALES TAX Provincial sales tax on every invoice An Ontario invoice carries HST, a Quebec invoice carries GST and QST, and other provinces apply GST with or without a provincial tax depending on what is sold. When this is a manual line edit, invoices go out wrong. We model the tax rule by province and sync the result to QuickBooks or Xero, so finance reviews exceptions instead of every invoice. Accounting integrations HOURS ELD data where dispatch can use it Federally regulated Canadian carriers must use certified ELDs. That data is far more useful on the dispatch screen than in a separate portal. Where your ELD provider offers an API, we show remaining hours on the assignment row, so planning respects the limit rather than discovering it mid-trip.
