Data sources, hierarchy, and build scope, before the first call. 01 Can you improve a dashboard we already have? Often, yes. If your TMS or reporting tool lets us control layout and filters, we redesign inside it. If it does not, we say so and show what a custom view would cost in time. Either way we start by watching how the dashboard is used today. 02 Where does the data come from, and how fresh is it? From the systems you already run: the TMS, CRM, accounting tool, and telematics if the vendor offers an API. A dashboard is only as current as its slowest source, so we agree refresh timing per source and show "last updated" on screen. We do not promise live data we cannot get. 03 Can a dashboard replace our morning-scan spreadsheet? That is the most common reason people call. If the spreadsheet exists because filters and actions do not match the work, a redesigned view can retire it. ShiftRail is the example: the desk moved off a second sheet once exceptions and SLA sat on the board. 04 How do you decide what goes at the top? Each role gets one primary question, such as which loads will miss their appointment. The top of the screen answers that question and lets the user act. A chart that does not change a decision moves lower or goes away. 05 Do you only design, or also build? Both are possible. Some teams want designs and specs for their own engineers; others want us to build the views too. We scope the data work separately because it is often the larger part. 06 What do you need from us? Screenshots or exports of what people use today, access to the data sources or sample exports, and time with two or three people who use the dashboard daily. A named person who can settle disagreements between roles helps a lot. START A CONVERSATION Show us the report your team rebuilds in a spreadsheet every morning.
