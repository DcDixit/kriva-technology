SaaS solutions OPERATING CONDITIONS What makes US trucking builds different. US carriers and brokers run inside a federal compliance layer that most generic CRMs ignore. The work that matters is getting hours, fuel, and load data into the same record the dispatcher already uses. We build the screens and data model that record these obligations; we do not give legal or tax advice, and your compliance owner signs off the rules. We pull remaining hours from your ELD provider’s API where one exists and show it on the assignment row, so the dispatcher sees the constraint before booking the load rather than after the driver calls in. Dispatch CRM 02 · MILES IFTA and mileage records Quarterly IFTA filing goes wrong when miles by state live in a spreadsheet someone rebuilds every quarter. A custom build can store jurisdiction miles and fuel purchases against the trip as they happen, with an export your accountant or filing tool can use, and a clear flag on trips with missing data. Workflow automation 03 · DOCUMENTS Broker, carrier, and factoring flow Rate confirmation, proof of delivery, and the invoice that goes to a factoring company are three documents describing one load. We link them to the same record, so billing does not wait on someone matching PDFs by hand, and the carrier pay status is visible to the desk.
