FAQ About how we work Straight answers so the first call starts further along. Book a free 20-minute fit call → 01 How long does a typical project take? Discovery is 1–2 weeks and ends with a brief you sign. Most scoped projects run 6–16 weeks. A larger SaaS platform with integrations can run 12–20 weeks. We do not quote a date until the brief is written. A fit call is for fit, not a timeline we cannot yet defend. 02 What do you need from us? A named decision-maker, access to the people who will use the product, and feedback inside the weekly cadence. If the desk cannot join a demo, we will say the engagement is not ready. 03 How do timezones work? We work remote-first. The studio is in Ahmedabad, India, with overlap windows for UK and US clients. Demos sit in that overlap. Async notes go out the same day. You should not need midnight calls. 04 How is this different from AI-Assisted Development? This page is the delivery sequence: phases, gates, and cadence. AI-assisted development is how tools such as Claude, Copilot, and Cursor are used inside those phases. Tools speed the work. A person still signs what ships. 05 Do QuickBooks and Xero projects use the same process? Yes. Finance connectors still get a written brief, a signed operator surface, weekly demos, and a go-live checklist. The write into QuickBooks Online or Xero uses the original APIs. The gates do not change. 06 What happens after launch? You can take the handoff and run it, or stay on a monthly retainer for iteration and automation maintenance. Support is Phase 05 and optional.
