AT A GLANCE What a QuickBooks or Xero integration should do A QuickBooks or Xero integration moves financial records from your product into your accounting ledger automatically: invoices, payments, credit notes, customers, and the tax and account codes behind them. A good one also proves the two sides agree. KRIVA builds the connector on the public Intuit and Xero APIs, a match step that compares amounts, tax, entity, and currency, and an exception queue where finance resolves anything that does not match before close. 01 Who it is for SaaS and operations platforms that already invoice, collect, or pay out, and need those movements to land cleanly in QuickBooks Online or Xero, including teams with books in more than one country. 02 What we build The connector, data mapping, matching, retry rules, and the operator screens finance uses when a line does not match. Design and engineering come from the same in-house team. 03 What this page is not CRM, payment, and other operational connectors are covered under API integrations. No-code Zapier or Make workflows are covered under workflow automation. Our bar: the integration is not finished when data syncs. It is finished when finance can close the books with confidence. PLATFORMS The two ledgers we build for QuickBooks Online and Xero, through their public APIs. A common setup is QuickBooks for US entities and Xero for UK or Australian ones. When you run both, the two connectors share one operator view. QuickBooks Online Intuit API Xero Xero API QuickBooks Online INTUIT API QuickBooks Online is the usual ledger for US books. We map invoices, payments, customers, items, and tax codes through Intuit's public API, with OAuth 2.0 token refresh, backoff when Intuit throttles requests (HTTP 429), and writes keyed to your source records so a retry cannot post twice. Month-end is where it breaks. These are the three failure patterns we design against from the first week. 01 Silent drift Jobs report success while amounts, tax, or entity mapping quietly diverge. Controllers find the difference in a spreadsheet, not in the product. Cost: variances surface outside the product 02 Errors finance cannot act on Exceptions pile up as raw API errors with no invoice, customer, or amount attached. An outsourced bookkeeper cannot fix them without engineering, so the close slips. Cost: close waits on an engineering ticket 03 Retries that post twice A request times out, the job retries, and the same invoice lands in the ledger twice. Without a key tied to the source record, every retry can make the books worse.
