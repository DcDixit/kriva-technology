WHERE IT HAS TO WORK Test the mark where it will live. A carrier mark may be applied to vinyl and printed on forms. A software mark lives in navigation bars, app icons, and invoices. We design for the surfaces yours will actually meet. 32px FAVICON AND BROWSER TAB 1× ONE-COLOR DECALS AND STAMPS Dark CONSOLE AND APP HEADER WHERE MARKS FAIL The moment it has to scale. 01 · SIZE Detail disappears Fine lines that look good on a letterhead vanish at 32 pixels and on a small decal. 02 · WIDTH The wordmark is too long A long name does not fit an app header or a square icon, and nobody planned a short form. 03 · BACKGROUND It only works on white The color version breaks on dark dashboards and photos, and there is no inverse. WHAT YOU RECEIVE More than a PNG. MARK FILES Vector artwork in SVG, plus PNG exports at the sizes you need. LOCKUPS Horizontal, stacked, and icon-only versions, so the mark fits a header and a square. COLOR VARIANTS Full color, one-color, and inverse versions that hold on light and dark backgrounds. USAGE SHEET Clear space, minimum size, and a short list of misuses your team and vendors can follow. HOW IT RUNS Brief, explore, stress test, package. 01 Brief and audit We look at your current mark, where it is used, and the marks of competitors your buyers compare you with. You provide: existing files, a list of surfaces, and any mandatory markings. 02 Explore A small number of directions based on the brief. We settle one in a review session, not a wall of options. 03 Stress test The chosen mark is checked at favicon size, as an app icon, in an invoice header, in one color, and on a dark interface before it is refined. 04 Package Final files, lockups, variants, and the usage sheet. If a branding or product engagement follows, the mark carries straight into it. SCOPE AND LIMITS What logo design does not include. The scope is the mark and its usage rules. Trademark clearance. We do not search registers or give legal advice. Vehicle regulatory markings. Required numbers and legal lettering are your responsibility and your sign shop’s. Printing and application. We hand over artwork; your vendors produce and apply it. A full visual identity. Color systems, type, and templates beyond the mark are branding. RELATED WORK Mark, then system.
