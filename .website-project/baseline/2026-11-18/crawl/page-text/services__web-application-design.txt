TRUCKING, BROKERAGE, AND LOGISTICS Portals for people outside your four walls Carriers who need load offers, documents, and pay status without calling dispatch Shippers and customers who want shipment history, documents, and invoices in one place Brokers and dispatchers who work from a load board all day B2B SAAS The daily app, not the sign-up page Workspace navigation, roles, and permission screens that stay readable as accounts grow Settings, billing, and admin views that support teams can explain Empty, loading, and error states designed with the main flow THE SCREENS THAT MATTER Four screens decide whether the app is used. 01 · LOAD LIST Carrier load offers Offer details, accept or decline, and a clear next step. Rate, pickup window, and equipment are visible without opening a second page. 02 · DOCUMENTS BOL, POD, and invoice status Upload from a phone, see what is missing, and see where a document sits in review. Carriers should not have to email a dispatcher to learn that a file arrived. 03 · SHIPMENTS Customer shipment view Status, documents, and exception notes for each shipment, with a plain way to ask a question or raise a change request. 04 · LOAD BOARD Search, compare, act Saved filters, dense but scannable rows, keyboard-friendly actions, and states for no results, stale results, and a load that was taken while you looked. HOW WE WORK Roles and tasks first, then screens. 01 · MAP Roles, tasks, and permissions Who signs in, what each role must finish, and what each role must never see. For carriers and customers this is where the portal's scope gets honest. 02 · DESIGN The dense states, not just the happy path Tables, filters, bulk actions, empty and error states, and permission edges, designed in Figma at desktop and tablet widths. 03 · PROTOTYPE AND SPEC A clickable path your team can argue over once One prototype for stakeholders, then annotated specs and components for engineering.
