WHEN OPINIONS REPLACE EVIDENCE The loudest stakeholder is not a sample. We watch the people who use the product, test the paths they actually take, and deliver a readout your team can act on, not a long appendix. WHAT WE STUDY The desk, the cab, and the customer. TRUCKING AND LOGISTICS Dispatch desks, drivers, customer service How dispatchers triage exceptions across phone, text, and the console Where drivers stall when accepting a load, updating status, or uploading a document Why customers call to ask where a load is instead of using tracking How carriers move through onboarding and where they drop out B2B SAAS Activation, onboarding, and daily use First-session tests: what a new admin does in the first thirty minutes Interviews with admins and end users on roles and permissions Where support tickets cluster, and what they say about the product Prototype tests before a redesign is built METHODS Chosen for the question, not the template. 01 · SHADOWING Contextual observation Sit with a dispatch desk or an ops team during real work, remotely or on a call, and record the workarounds, interruptions, and handoffs. 02 · INTERVIEWS Role-based conversations Structured interviews per role, so dispatcher, driver, and customer-service findings stay separate until the synthesis. 03 · USABILITY Task tests on the real path Tasks against the live product or a prototype. We record where people stall, not whether they like the look. 04 · EVIDENCE REVIEW Tickets, call notes, and journeys Support tickets and call notes you share, mapped into journeys so patterns come from more than the interviews. THE SPRINT Most focused studies run 2-4 weeks. 01 · PLAN Questions, screener, and participants We agree on the decisions the research must inform, write the screener, and schedule sessions. Recruitment pace sets the calendar. 02 · FIELD Shadow, interview, and test Sessions per role, each recorded with permission and tagged against the questions we planned. 03 · READOUT Decisions, not theatre Journey maps and a ranked list of what to change before the UI is locked, presented to the team that has to act on it. WHAT YOU GET, AND THE LIMITS What to build. What to cut. What to test again. 01 Journey maps per role 02 Usability findings tied to tasks 03 Ranked recommendations 04 Recordings and notes you own Honest limits Research reduces risk. It does not remove it, and it does not ship interface work. Small samples: a handful of sessions per role gives direction, not statistical proof. Access: findings are only as good as the people we can reach, so we flag whose voice is missing. Privacy: we observe under NDA and do not record drivers while they are driving. Next step: design work follows in UI/UX design or prototyping.
