Short answers on scope, timelines, cost, and what we need before a first call. Photograph via Pexels License. Not a KRIVA client. 01 Should we customize our CRM or replace it? We assess first. If stages, permissions, and the CRM's own bulk actions already fit, configuration is the answer and nothing needs building. Replacement comes up only when the platform cannot support bulk work, role-based views, or audited overrides for the way your desk runs. 02 Will this replace our TMS? Not by default. The TMS stays the system of record and the new console runs beside it, rolled out by role or module. If you want to retire a TMS module later, we scope that as a separate step. 03 Can the dispatch board work with our TMS? Usually, depending on what the TMS exposes. We check API access, export options, and your account permissions first. If there is no usable API, scheduled exports or an import step are the fallback, with more failure modes. We tell you before the build which case you are in. 04 Can you migrate data from a legacy CRM or spreadsheets? Yes, when it is in scope. We map fields, run test imports on sanitized samples, and agree a cutover by role or module so the desk keeps working. Messy history sometimes needs a decision from your team about what to keep. 05 What do you need from us to start? A day of watching the desk work, a sanitized export of recent loads or deals, sandbox or admin access to the CRM and TMS, and one person per role who can say whether a screen works for them. 06 What happens after launch? Designs, code, and documentation transfer to your accounts. Changes after go-live can stay with your team, or we can agree a support retainer if you want one. We do not host your data unless we scope hosting as part of the project. 07 How long does a custom CRM project take? Discovery takes 1 to 2 weeks and ends with a scoped brief and timeline you sign off. A focused first version, such as one console or one pipeline, is usually 6 to 10 weeks after that. A larger build with several integrations usually runs 12 to 20 weeks. 08 How much does custom CRM development cost? Cost follows scope: team size multiplied by weeks. We price after discovery, as a fixed-scope project, sprints, or a monthly retainer. Our guide to custom dispatch software cost explains what drives the budget.
