How to choose a budget 1 SMALL CUSTOM SITE Need a phone number, authority, and a simple quote form: a small custom site is enough. 2 SHIPPER AND DRIVER PAGES Need different pages for shippers and drivers: budget for more than a one-page template. 3 SOFTWARE Need customers to track a load, or drivers to upload documents: you are buying software. Read dispatch software cost before you add that to a website quote. 3 Ask every vendor the same three questions: how many pages, what the form captures, and whether you own the site when the invoice is paid.
