Scope, tenant isolation, integrations, and how we ship into a product your customers already use. 01 Do you build the platform, or only design it? Both. One in-house team designs and engineers the tenant model, the admin and customer surfaces, and the APIs and integrations behind them, typically in Next.js, React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. If your engineers prefer to write the code, we deliver the role model, designs, and component specs instead. Either way, you own the code and designs. 02 Can you change a live SaaS product without a big-bang cutover? Yes, and phased is the default. New surfaces and permission checks ship behind feature flags, tested in staging, and roll out to pilot tenants first. Existing tenants keep working while the rest of the product moves over. 03 How do you keep one tenant's data away from another? Every record carries its tenant, and the API checks tenant and role on every read and write instead of relying on filters in the UI. We review where those rules are enforced today and agree the isolation model with your engineers before migrating anything. 04 Can you integrate SSO, subscriptions, and billing? Yes. We connect SSO sign-in to the role model, with a safe default role for new users, and build plan, seat, and billing screens inside the product. Subscription and payment events from Stripe or your billing system can post to QuickBooks Online or Xero with an exception queue for finance (see accounting integrations). We confirm the exact providers during discovery. 05 How is this different from SaaS product development? SaaS product development covers new products: MVP scope, onboarding, and activation. This service covers live platforms that already have tenants, roles, and billing, where the work is tenant isolation, permissions, and bringing admin and customer surfaces back into one product family. 06 What do you need from us to start? Access to the live product or a staging copy, read access to the repository, your current role and permission list, a few sanitized tenant setups, and time with someone from support or customer success who hears where tenants get stuck.
