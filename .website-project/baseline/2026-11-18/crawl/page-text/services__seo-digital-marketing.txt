Who SEO is for, what people search for, and what we deliver Who it’s for Carriers, brokers, and 3PLs with a marketing site, and freight-tech and SaaS vendors with product pages, whose site is hard to find or hard to measure. Typically teams without an in-house search specialist. What people search Buyers comparing dispatch or TMS software, operators checking whether a tool integrates with QuickBooks or Xero, and shippers vetting a carrier. These are examples of intent, not a keyword list we promise to win. What we deliver A crawl and index audit, page structure mapped to buyer questions, page briefs, schema, and GA4 and Search Console set up so form fills and calls show up as events. KRIVA is a product design and engineering studio. SEO is a supporting service, strongest when it sits next to a site or product we build. You keep account access and documentation. WHERE IT BREAKS Three common gaps on logistics and SaaS sites. We name the failure before we publish another page. 01 Product pages describe features, not the job A TMS or dispatch page that never says what a dispatcher or broker is trying to do gives search engines and buyers little to match. COST: THE RIGHT BUYER CANNOT TELL THE PAGE IS FOR THEM 02 Thin, duplicated lane and service pages Near-identical pages for each lane, city, or service, with duplicate URLs and no canonical. COST: PAGES COMPETE WITH EACH OTHER 03 Quote requests are not tracked as events Form fills, calls, and demo requests are not recorded in GA4, or they fire twice.
