We design and build dispatch tools, fleet software, SaaS products, and accounting integrations that operators and product teams use on a real workday. The team spent years inside agencies and product companies watching the same pattern. Design handed off specs engineering could not build. Clients managed three vendors for one product. Interfaces looked finished in Figma and fell apart in production. KRIVA started in 2025 so a client can hire one in-house team. The founder leads design. Nine years is the experience that team brings from earlier product work, not the age of the company. We work with teams in the United States, the United Kingdom, the UAE, Canada, and Australia. Headquarters is in Ahmedabad. We do not have local offices in those markets.
