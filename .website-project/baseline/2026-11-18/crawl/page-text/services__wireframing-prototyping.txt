WHAT WE MAP The flows where misunderstandings get expensive. 01 · DISPATCH AND EXCEPTIONS Assign, reassign, escalate Bulk assignment, SLA risk on the row, supervisor overrides, and what the desk sees when a load goes wrong. 02 · DRIVER APP Accept, update, upload Load acceptance, status changes, document capture, and what happens when the signal drops. 03 · PORTAL AND ONBOARDING Quote, track, set up Customer tracking, quotes, and SaaS first-run, including roles and permissions that are missing. WHY BEFORE CODE Production is a slow way to learn the flow is wrong. 01 · EXCEPTIONS Edges show up after sprint one Error, empty, and permission states were left for later. Later is now the backlog. 02 · ROLES Dispatcher, supervisor, and driver want different things Without a walkable prototype, each role is designed in a meeting by whoever speaks first. 03 · ROLLOUT The old TMS and spreadsheet are still running A prototype shows how the new tool coexists with the system of record before anything is built. WHAT YOU RECEIVE Material the next team can build from. FLOW MAP The path for each role, from trigger to outcome, including decision points. WIREFRAME SET Boxes and labels for each screen. No color standing in for a decision. CLICKABLE PROTOTYPE A Figma prototype of the agreed flows, including failure states and empty screens. STATE INVENTORY A list of states, edge cases, and open questions to resolve before design or build. HOW IT RUNS Map, wire, click through, hand off. 01 Map the workflow We walk through how the work happens today. You provide: screenshots or exports of the current tool or spreadsheet, a list of roles, and time with one or two operators. 02 Wire the structure Low-fidelity wireframes until the path is agreed. Structure first, visual styling later. 03 Click through, including edges Stakeholders walk failed states and empty screens, not only the happy path. We note every decision this forces. 04 Hand off The prototype and state inventory become the brief for UI design or engineering, not a workshop artifact that gets discarded. SCOPE AND LIMITS What a prototype will not tell you. It reduces guesswork, and it has limits. Not production UI. Wireframes are structural. Visual design is UI/UX design. Not integration proof. A prototype does not test your TMS, ELD, or accounting data. Not a research study. Walkthroughs are small and directional; larger studies are UX research. Not a build estimate. It sharpens the scope, but engineering estimates come from engineering. RELATED WORK Two published cases. Neither case page publishes outcome figures on prototyping itself.
