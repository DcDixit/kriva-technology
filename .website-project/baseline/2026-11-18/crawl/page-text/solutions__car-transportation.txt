CORE CHALLENGES One order should not become five disconnected workflows. When quoting, dispatch, and billing each keep their own copy of the order, the office spends its day reconciling instead of moving cars. Customer-facing 02 ASSIGN AND DISPATCH Capacity, route, and equipment in one assignment view. Changes and exceptions are handled on the order, not in a text thread. Ops console 03 PICKUP AND TRANSIT Condition capture protects both sides. The driver records photos, existing damage, and whether the vehicle runs, often one-handed in a driveway. If signal drops, updates queue on the phone and sync when it returns. At pickup, the condition report is the record both sides rely on. Driver app 04 DELIVER AND CLOSE POD closes the order and starts the invoice. A signature and delivery photos close the move.
