Who graphic design is for, what we deliver, and what it is not Who it’s for Marketing leads and founders at carriers, brokers, freight-tech vendors, and SaaS companies whose decks, rate sheets, and social posts have drifted from the brand. Teams that want a kit, not a one-time file. What we deliver Sales and investor decks, capability one-pagers, trade-show and print graphics, email and social templates, and workflow diagrams, as editable source files with a short use guide. What it isn’t An identity from scratch (that is branding), a monthly production desk, or print management. We build the kit; your team produces the day-to-day pieces from it. KRIVA is a product design and engineering studio. Graphic design is a supporting service, usually paired with branding or a product launch. You own the source files and templates after delivery. WHERE IT BREAKS When collateral is rebuilt every quarter. The deck, the one-pager, and the product should read as one company.
