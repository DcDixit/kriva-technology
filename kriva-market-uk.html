SaaS product design

Operating conditions

What makes UK SaaS and logistics builds different.

UK products tend to meet tax, data-protection, and transport rules earlier than founders expect, often at the first enterprise customer. Designing for them from the start is cheaper than retrofitting. We build the screens and data model that record these obligations; we do not give legal or tax advice, and your compliance owner signs off the rules.

  • Making Tax Digital-friendly records when finance workflows touch VAT
  • Xero-first accounting integration, with QuickBooks where needed
  • UK GDPR: data export, deletion, and audit trails in the admin panel
  • Drivers’ hours and tachograph data for operators with HGV fleets
  • GB and EU loads on one desk, with customs status where it applies

Tax

Xero and Making Tax Digital

Making Tax Digital for VAT expects digital records with a digital link between systems, not figures retyped from one tool into another. When your product creates invoices or expenses, we sync them to Xero or QuickBooks through official APIs, so the VAT figures in your accounting tool trace back to source records.

Data protection

UK GDPR in the admin panel

Enterprise buyers ask early how a customer’s data is exported, corrected, and deleted, and who saw it. We build these as real admin features: role-based access, an audit log, and export and deletion flows. They answer a security questionnaire with screenshots rather than promises.

Transport

Operator licence and drivers’ hours data

UK haulage operators work under an operator licence and drivers’ hours rules recorded on tachographs. Dispatch tools that ignore this let a desk plan work a driver cannot legally do. We bring hours and vehicle data into the planning view, so the conflict appears before the job is assigned.
