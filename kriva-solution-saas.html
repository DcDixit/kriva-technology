SaaS product design

Signup is not the product.

We design the first run so B2B SaaS users reach a real outcome — not an empty dashboard and a product tour.

Book a 20-minute fit call→ See PayrollPro→

Onboarding · first meaningful action

Home · the morning scan, not a metrics museum

Where signup leaks

Users signed up. Then they left.

Activation is not “completed setup.” It is the first outcome they came for. Most products lose people in the same three places.

01

An empty dashboard

The account exists. The workspace is named. Nothing useful is on screen, so a tooltip tour stands in for the product.

02

Permissions before value

SSO and role sprawl push admin setup ahead of the job. The buyer never reaches the moment that proves the product.

03

No shared “activated”

Growth, product, and design track different events. Nobody can say what first value is — so nobody can design for it.

What we design

The surfaces that decide whether people stay.

Onboarding, roles, and the daily home — designed as one first-run, not three products bolted together.

First value before the tour ends

Collect only what creates a workspace. Route the checklist from role and intent. Design the empty state as a path, not a blank canvas.

  • Progressive setup matched to who is signing in
  • One guided action that delivers the job they came for
  • Activation events product and design can both name
SaaS product design →

Roles people can explain out loud

Multi-tenant access has to stay understandable when SSO and enterprise policy arrive. Default safe roles. Defer advanced admin until someone needs it.

  • Plain-language roles before policy matrices
  • Invite flows that match how teams actually join
  • Admin that does not block first value

A home for the morning scan

Dashboards and admin panels designed for exceptions and next actions — density that holds up in a demo without becoming a metrics museum.

  • Hierarchy for what needs a decision today
  • Patterns that survive new modules and roles
  • Demo-ready without visual noise

How we engage
