When mobile is a shrunk desktop

The app installs. Usage does not stick.

These are the patterns we see before a redesign starts.

01 · Hand

Desktop shrink

Screens copied from the web miss thumb targets and fail outdoors. Operators text dispatch instead of opening the app.

02 · Value

Everything before the first action

Onboarding asks for the world. Interrupted users never reach accept, book, or update status.

03 · Store

Listing does not match the product

Installs happen. Activation and reviews do not, because the first session is not the job they came to finish.

Jobs on the hand

Three actions. Then everything else.

If accept, status, and documents work with one thumb, the rest of the product can stay quiet.

Accept without a call

Lane, time, and the decision sit in the first viewport. Secondary detail waits. Gloves, sun, and a 20-second window.

  • Primary CTA in the thumb zone
  • Decline is visible, not hidden in a menu
  • Load identity readable at arm’s length

Status that dispatch can trust

One tap to the next state. Exceptions are a reason, not a free-text novel. The board updates without a phone call.

  • States in the order the desk already uses
  • Exception codes before a comment field
  • Offline queue when the yard has no signal

Documents at the gate

Capture, confirm, done. The camera is the product, not a buried attachment picker copied from the web app.

  • Photo-first for POD and inspection
  • What’s still open, not a file tree
  • Retry when the upload fails in the lot

Approach

Design for interruption. Then platform.

Task flows first. Native patterns where they matter. Store listing last, so it matches the product that ships.

  1. 01 · Jobs

    Name the first action

    We write the job that must succeed in 20 seconds. Everything else waits, including account setup copied from the web.

  2. 02 · Hand

    Thumb, glare, gloves

    Targets, contrast, and density for the cab or the clinic hallway. iOS and Android stay native without inventing two products.

  3. 03 · Ship

    Build path, then store

    React Native, Flutter, or native — chosen with your constraints. Listing and screenshots match the first session, not a pitch deck.
