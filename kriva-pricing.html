Scope drivers

What changes the final number

The proposal lists what is included, what is excluded, and the timeline. If a number is much lower than the starting prices above, ask what was left out.

Roles

How many roles use the system, and whether drivers or customers get their own surface.

Integrations

Whether the other system has a documented API and a test account we can use during build.

Content

What you can provide versus copy and photography we have to produce before launch.

Cutover

Whether the first release must replace the current tool or can run beside it for a while.

How we price

Why a US onshore quote is often higher

KRIVA uses AI-assisted tools for repetitive production work. Design decisions, workflow mapping, code review, and testing stay with the team. The tools shorten some hours. They do not remove the hours spent learning how your desk works.

We do not promise a percentage off a US agency quote. The written estimate after a call is the price.
