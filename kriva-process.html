Process

Five phases.Visible progress.

Discovery, design, build, launch, and support. Each phase has milestones, weekly demos, and deliverables you sign off before we move on. Tools help us move faster — people own every decision.

How a project runs

You always know what you're approving next.

Discover

Phase 01 You sign off

We capture goals, users, constraints, and success metrics in a written brief before any design starts.

Design

Phase 02 You sign off

Flows, prototypes, and high-fidelity UI reviewed by stakeholders and signed off before development begins.

Build

Phase 03

Incremental delivery with weekly demos, QA gates, and open progress tracking. No surprises.

Launch

Phase 04

Deployment, analytics setup, handoff documentation, and team training.

Support

Phase 05

Iteration sprints, automation maintenance, and roadmap support. Most clients stay on a monthly retainer after launch.
