How a project runs

Five phases. Hard gates.

We do not start the next phase on a handshake. Discover and Design have hard sign-off. Build has weekly demos. Launch has a go-live checklist. Support is optional and named.

The brief

Phase 01 · 1–2 weeks · you sign off

Written next step Named owner No handshake gates
  1. W1–2

    Discover

    Phase 01 · 1–2 weeks You sign off

    Goals, users, constraints, and success metrics in a written brief. We sit with the desk that will use the product, not only the sponsor who commissioned it.

    You leave withA scoped brief and a realistic timeline

  2. W3–7

    Design

    Phase 02 · 2–5 weeks You sign off

    Flows, prototypes, and high-fidelity UI reviewed in weekly demos. Engineering does not start until the pack is signed. Changes after this gate are a change request, not a surprise.

    You leave withClickable UI the floor can react to

  3. Weekly

    Build

    Phase 03 · weekly demos

    Incremental delivery with QA gates and open progress. You see working software every week, not a big reveal. Tools accelerate the work. A person still reviews what ships.

    You leave withShippable increments, not a black box

  4. Go-live

    Launch

    Phase 04 Go-live checklist

    Deployment, analytics, handoff docs, and training for the people who will run it. We do not call it live until the checklist is green and someone on your side can operate it.

    You leave withA live product and a named owner

  5. Optional

    Support

    Phase 05 · optional retainer

    Iteration sprints, automation maintenance, and roadmap support. Most clients stay on a monthly retainer after launch. It is a choice, not a lock-in buried in the SOW.

    You leave withA clear retain-or-handoff decision

Cadence

How the week actually feels

Ahmedabad, India. Remote-first. Overlap windows for UK and US clients so the demo is a conversation, not a recording.

01

Weekly demo, written next step

Every week you see the work and leave with the next decision named. No status theater. If a gate is blocked, we say so on the call.

02

UK and US hours, on purpose

Demos sit in the overlap. Async updates land in writing the same day. You are not expected to stay up for India midnight.

03

One owner on each side

You name a decision-maker. We name a lead. Feedback that is not from the owner waits. That is how sign-off stays real.
