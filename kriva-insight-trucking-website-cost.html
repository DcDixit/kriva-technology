How much does a trucking company website cost?

Published US prices in 2026 run from a few hundred dollars for a template to the mid five figures when the site includes portals. The useful question is which of those products you are buying.

November 14, 2026 8 min read KRIVA Technologies

Carriers ask for a website price and receive answers that are not describing the same work. This note separates the published offers we could verify from what a custom KRIVA site includes. Figures from other companies are theirs, checked in November 2026, and will move.

What KRIVA’s starting price includes

A trucking, moving, or logistics website starts at $4,800. That scope is a working session, custom design, up to six pages, a mobile layout, one quote or contact form, on-page titles and headings, and launch on your domain. Photography, extra pages, a content editor, and anything behind a login are outside it.

That price sits with the professional custom builds above, not with the $899 packages. It is lower than many US agency quotes for a multi-audience carrier site, and it is not a claim that every project lands at $4,800. The pricing page lists what moves the number. The page we would build is described under trucking company website design.

How to choose a budget

  • Need a phone number, authority, and a simple quote form: a small custom site is enough.
  • Need different pages for shippers and drivers: budget for more than a one-page template.
  • Need customers to track a load, or drivers to upload documents: you are buying software. Read dispatch software cost before you add that to a website quote.

Ask every vendor the same three questions: how many pages, what the form captures, and whether you own the site when the invoice is paid. KRIVA’s answer on ownership is yes. The studio is in Ahmedabad and works remotely with US carriers.

Questions we hear before a quote

What does KRIVA charge for a trucking website?

A defined website of up to six pages, custom design, one form, and launch starts at $4,800. Tracking portals, driver applications with a login, and TMS connections are quoted separately.

Why do other sites advertise $899 trucking websites?

Those offers are usually a small page count, a template, or a monthly plan. They can be the right buy for an owner-operator who needs a phone number online. They are a different product from a custom site with separate shipper and driver paths.
