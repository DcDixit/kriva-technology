Service · Brand identity

Brand identity for logistics and software teams.

An identity that holds on a trailer door, a rate confirmation, a sales deck, and a product login screen. Built for carriers, brokers, freight-tech vendors, and the SaaS teams we also build software for.

Branding is a supporting service at KRIVA. Most of our work is trucking and logistics software, SaaS products, and integrations. We take on brand work when it sits next to a product, or when a software or logistics company needs its identity to stop contradicting its product.

What you receive

Rules a product team and a marketing team can both follow.

Vehicle, trailer, and uniform artwork is delivered as vector files. Your sign shop or wrap vendor produces them.

  1. A one-page statement of who the brand is for, what it promises, and the tone it uses. Written with you and short enough to actually use.

  2. A refined or new mark, color with pairings checked for contrast, a type pair and scale, and layout and spacing rules.

  3. A document covering logo use, color, type, imagery, and tone, including the digital values engineers need.

  4. A sales deck, a one-pager, an email signature, and social headers built on the rules, so the next asset does not start from zero.

Scope and limits

What this service does not cover.

Brand work here has clear edges, so the engagement stays about the identity.

  • 01

    Naming and trademark. We can react to name options, but we do not run trademark searches or give legal clearance. Check with counsel.

  • 02

    Production. We do not print, wrap, or manufacture. Artwork goes to your vendors.

  • 03

    Ad campaigns. Brand work stops at the identity and templates. Search work is separate: see SEO and digital marketing.

    Market outcomes. A clearer brand helps people recognize you. We do not promise leads or conversion changes.
