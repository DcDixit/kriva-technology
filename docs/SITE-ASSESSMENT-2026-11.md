## Pages deliberately not removed

No URL was removed or redirected, including low-demand service pages. There is no Search Console data yet to justify it, and removal is hard to reverse once Google has processed it.

## Location pages

No US state, UK region, Australian state, or UAE emirate pages were created. KRIVA has no local office or local proof to put on them, so they would be the thin, repetitive pages the brief ruled out. Revisit when real client work exists in a specific place, and when Search Console shows demand there.
