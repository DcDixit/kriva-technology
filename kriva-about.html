Our story

Why we exist.

We design and build dispatch tools, fleet software, SaaS dashboards, and accounting integrations that operators and product teams actually want to use.

The team has spent years designing and shipping software inside agencies and product companies where the same problems repeated. Design handed off specs engineering could not build. Clients managed three vendors for one product. Interfaces looked finished in Figma and fell apart in production.

KRIVA started in 2025 to be a studio where design and engineering work together from day one, where the person who designs the interface understands the API it connects to, and where clients talk to the people doing the work.

We are based in Ahmedabad and work with teams in the United States, United Kingdom, UAE, and Canada. What those teams have in common: they need software built by people who have already solved problems like theirs.

How we operate

What working with us feels like.

Four operating rules. They are how the studio runs, and what you should expect after the first call.

01

One conversation

Design and engineering sit in the same thread from day one. No handoff blame, no “that’s not my department.”

02

Show the work weekly

Demos on a real URL, written tradeoffs, and shared access to the same tools we use. We say when something is not working, not only when it is.

03

AI for speed, people for ship

Claude, Cursor, and Figma AI where they actually help. Human review before anything reaches production or a customer.

04

Built to last without us

Clean Figma files, typed code, and documentation your team can use after we leave, not artifacts that only make sense to us.
