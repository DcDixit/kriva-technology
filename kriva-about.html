Our story

Why we exist.

We design and build dispatch tools, fleet software, SaaS dashboards, and accounting integrations that operators and product teams actually want to use.

The team has spent years designing and shipping software: SaaS dashboards, trucking dispatch tools, QuickBooks integrations, mobile apps. Most of that work happened inside agencies and product companies where the same problems repeated. Design handed off specs engineering couldn't build. Clients managed three vendors for one product. Interfaces looked finished in Figma and fell apart in production.

KRIVA started in 2025 to be a studio where design and engineering work together from day one, where the person who designs the interface understands the API it connects to, and where clients talk to the people doing the work, not a project manager relaying messages.

We're based in Ahmedabad, India. Our clients are in the US, the UK, and across the world. What they have in common: they need software built by people who've already solved problems like theirs.

How we operate

What working with us feels like.

Four operating rules. They are how the studio runs, and what you should expect after the first call.

01

One conversation

Design and engineering sit in the same thread from day one. No handoff blame, no “that’s not my department.”

02

Show the work weekly

Demos on a real URL, written tradeoffs, and shared access to the same tools we use. We say when something isn’t working, not only when it is.

03

AI for speed, people for ship

Claude, Cursor, and Figma AI where they actually help. Human review before anything reaches production or a customer.

04

Built to last without us

Clean Figma files, typed code, and documentation your team can use after we leave, not artifacts that only make sense to us.

Questions we get first

Before the call.

Who will I talk to?

Someone from the KRIVA team is on the first call, the people who will ship the work, not a salesperson. The designers and engineers on the project join when scoping starts.

How old is the company?

KRIVA Technologies was founded in 2025. Nine years refers to the team's experience designing and shipping product software, not the age of the studio. We keep those two facts separate on purpose.

Where are you based, and how do US and UK hours work?

Headquarters is 511 - I The Address, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380060, IN. The studio is remote-first. We cover US and UK call hours with India delivery overlap, and we reply within one business day.

Do you subcontract?

No. Design and engineering stay in-house. If a project needs a specialist we do not have, we say so before we start rather than quietly farming the work out.
