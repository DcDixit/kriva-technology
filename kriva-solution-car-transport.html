Core challenges

One order should not become five disconnected workflows

Most auto-transport platforms solve for one side of the business. The customer books, the carrier picks up, ops handles exceptions, and nothing connects cleanly.

Quote and Ops Disagree

The customer sees one price while ops must reconstruct lane, vehicle, and timing. That gap forces manual corrections, support calls, and lost conversions.

Carrier Coordination Lives Elsewhere

Assignment, driver updates, and exceptions move into texts and spreadsheets outside the platform. The console becomes reference only, not the source of truth.

Delivery Does Not Close the Workflow

POD capture, invoicing, and CRM updates still require separate manual steps. One delivery event should close the order—not trigger three systems.

Phase 01 · Quote and book

The quote form is the shop window.

Mobile-first entry for vehicle type, condition, origin, destination, pickup window, and payment. One unexplained price invites a competitor call. Customers need to see the breakdown and book with confidence.

Customer-facing
Phase 02 · Assign and dispatch

Slots, lane, and equipment in one assignment view.

Match the load to a carrier who can actually fulfill it: verified insurance, available slots, correct equipment, and pickup window. Exceptions stay on the console, not in messages. One dispatch notification cuts support volume more than any other message.

Ops console
Phase 03 · Pickup and transit

Condition capture protects both sides.

Photos, notes, running-or-not check, and driver status: in a driveway, daylight, one-handed. Weak signal behavior and offline queue ensure status updates queue and sync when signal returns. The condition report is the product at pickup.

Driver app
Phase 04 · Deliver and close

POD closes the order and starts the invoice.

Signature, photos, and delivery confirmation from one event: delivery, billing, CRM follow-ups, and accounting integration fire together. Customers see status and ETA updates before they ask. One shareable tracking link handles customer communication.
